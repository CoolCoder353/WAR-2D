# v0.13 UX design (Figma)

The v0.13 Bastion HUD: the Gun turret and Jammer build buttons in the Builder's build row, their hover cards, the two new pixel icons, the range rings shown while either is selected, the selected-building panel for each (with the turret's "Unpaid: not firing" state), and the alert rows they reuse. Same look as v0.7 to v0.12 (Jersey 10, square corners, the `Theme` and `Scale` variables, the existing `Panel`, `Command button` and `Button` components, `Icon/px/*` icon components). **No v0.13 UI production code is written until this file records the owner's approval.** A change to an approved frame goes back through Figma first.

- **File:** [WAR-2D v0.6 UX](https://www.figma.com/design/JrMColnJiQbFfODqyPjBYf), page **v0.13 Bastion** (node 88:2)
- **Status:** **Approved** by the owner on 2026-10-23 (build row: two rows by role)

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Icons.** The two drafts above: Gun turret with a red accent (muzzle and rivets: red like the other weapons, Bomb and Detonate), Jammer with a blue accent (waves: blue like the "sight" icons, Scout and Sniper). As before, a rejected icon gets a round of three options.
2. **Build row layout.** Nine buttons don't fit the 452 px card. Recommended: **B, two rows by role** (make: Miner, Barracks, Heavy Factory, Siege Workshop; defend: Wall, Bomb, Radar, Gun turret, Jammer), card width unchanged. Alternative: **A, one row**, the card widens to 558 px while Builders are selected. Plain flex-wrap (7 + 2, breaking after Radar) was not drawn as an option.
3. **Order:** Gun turret then Jammer, after Radar (spec: appended).
4. **Hover card for build buttons.** A card in the v0.12 production-tooltip style (name · cost, role line, then footprint, range or jam radius, and upkeep) for **every** build button, replacing today's plain `tooltip` text, so the two new role lines read the same way as the producers'. The alternative is to keep native tooltips and put the role line in the tooltip string ("Gun turret: shoots units, then buildings and walls").
5. **Rings.** Both rings in the Radar's style (owner accepted showing both): team colour, dashed, 6% fill. They show while the building is selected **and while placing a blueprint** (the spec only says "while selected"). When the building is unpaid or still a blueprint, the ring is drawn at 40% opacity (it isn't working).
6. **Selected-building panel gains two lines**: Upkeep (that building's running cost) and a status line. Turret: "Firing: range 7" / "Idle: range 7" (no target in reach) / "Unpaid: not firing" (danger). Jammer: "Jamming: radius 12" / "Unpaid: not jamming" (danger). Both under construction: "Blueprint: works when built" (secondary, not drawn). "Unpaid: not jamming" is an addition: the spec only names the turret's state.
7. **Several selected buildings of mixed types** keep today's chips; the Upkeep line shows the selection's total and the status line is hidden unless every selected building is a turret or every one a Jammer.
8. **Alerts:** no new alert kind. "Under attack near your Gun turret" and "Construction finished: Jammer" come from adding both names to `AlertFeedModel.BuildingName`.

# Quality-of-life UX design (Figma brief)

The player quality-of-life batch: sixteen changes to the HUD, the in-match menus and Settings. Same look as v0.6 to v0.14 (Jersey 10, square corners, the `Theme` and `Scale` variables, the existing `Panel`, `Command button`, `Button` and `Switch` components, `Icon/px/*` icons). **No production code for any change below is written until this file records the owner's approval.** A change to an approved frame goes back through Figma first.

- **File:** [WAR-2D v0.6 UX](https://www.figma.com/design/JrMColnJiQbFfODqyPjBYf), page **v0.15 QoL** (node 102:2)
- **Status:** **Approved by the owner on 2026-10-27** (page `v0.15 QoL – redesign`, node 116:2; the first drafts on `v0.15 QoL` 102:2 are superseded). Open decisions 13–20 are taken at the redesign's recommendation unless the owner says otherwise. Implementation is under way; see [Redesign](#redesign-page-v015-qol--redesign).
- **Numbering** follows the owner's request list (items 1 to 6, 8, 10 to 17, 19 of the 2026-10-27 QoL review).

## What already exists (so we don't redraw it)

- The top bar already has `income-value` ("+N/s income") and `upkeep-value` ("−N/s upkeep"). Item 4 builds on them.
- The selection panel already has `selection-upkeep` and `selection-status` (v0.13).
- Building buttons already have a plain `tooltip` attribute with just the name; v0.13 specified a hover card (`build-tooltip`) for them. Item 3 extends that card to every button.
- `Space` is not bound to anything. The alert feed already holds six alerts until dismissed, and clicking one centres the camera.
- Squad keys `1`–`0`, `Ctrl` + number, double-click "select all of this type on screen", `F` Repair, `G` Dig, `B` Detonate, `Delete` all exist.

## 1. Idle Builder, select all Builders, army

**Frame `Selection shortcuts`** (node ?). A new `quick-select` strip, left of the squad bar, same height as a squad slot, three chips with icons and a live count each:

- **Idle Builders** (`select-idle-builders`): Builder icon, count of own Builders with no order and no build target. Count 0: chip dimmed and not clickable. Count above 0: **the chip pulses once** when it goes from 0 to above 0 (never repeating, so it doesn't nag). Click or `.` (period) selects them; pressing again with them selected cycles the camera to the next one.
- **All Builders** (`select-all-builders`): Builder icon with a "∞" mark. `Ctrl` + `.`.
- **Army** (`select-army`): crossed-swords icon, count of own units that can attack (so no Builders, Diggers or bombers). `,` (comma).

The chip's tooltip names its key ("Idle Builders (.)"). An empty selection result (none exist) does nothing and shows "No idle Builders" as the cursor tag for 1 s.

Element names: `quick-select`, `select-idle-builders`, `select-all-builders`, `select-army`, each with a `…-count` label. New rebindable actions `SelectIdleBuilders`, `SelectAllBuilders`, `SelectArmy`.

*Why army excludes Diggers and bombers:* an attack-move with Diggers and bombers mixed in sends bombers into your own walls. A second tap on the Army chip adds them (Diggers, bombers).

## 2. Jump to HQ

**Frame `Top bar – HQ button`.** A new `hq-button` as the first button in `top-bar__actions`: the HQ's pixel icon and "HQ", with `Backspace` as its key in the tooltip. One press centres the camera on your HQ, a second press within 0.4 s (or double-click) also selects it. When the HQ is destroyed (spectating) the button is hidden. Replay viewer: not shown.

New rebindable action `JumpToHq`, default `Backspace`.

## 3. Hotkeys and cost on every command button

**Frame `Command card – hover cards`.** One hover card (`command-tooltip`, `command-tooltip-title`, `command-tooltip-key`, `command-tooltip-role`, `command-tooltip-detail`) for **every** button on the command card, not only the build row. It generalises the v0.13 `build-tooltip` and replaces the plain `tooltip` attribute. Layout, top to bottom:

1. **Title and key**: "Attack-move" with a key chip "A" on the right, in the key chip style the command buttons already draw.
2. **Role line**: one sentence ("Move and fight what you meet").
3. **Detail line in the secondary colour**: for buildings the cost, upkeep and footprint ("150 · upkeep 4/s · 1×1 · range 7"); for production types the unit's cost, build time, health, damage and range; for orders nothing.
4. **Disabled reason in danger red**, when the button is greyed out: "Need 150 (you have 90)", "Needs an Idle Builder selected", "Needs a Radar", etc.

Shown after 0.4 s of hover, placed above the button so it never covers the card, never wider than 280 px. The key chip shows the player's **current binding** (so a rebind updates the card), and the card ends with "Right-click: …" for buttons that have a second action.

Frames to draw: Move, Attack-move, Stop, Hold, Repair, Dig, Detonate, Cancel/Demolish, one build button (Gun turret, as v0.13), one production type (Heavy), one disabled state (unaffordable).

## 4. Net income and a low-funds warning

**Frame `Top bar – economy`**, four states of the `resources` block:

- **A. Healthy:** `resources-value` 1,240, `income-value` "+45/s income" in success green, `upkeep-value` "−38/s upkeep", and a new **net** readout `net-value` "+7/s net" in the same green.
- **B. Break-even or negative:** `net-value` "−12/s net" in warning amber and a small down arrow; the tooltip reads "Upkeep is higher than income: units and buildings will decay when you run out".
- **C. Running low:** negative net and the balance would run out in under 60 s: `net-value` becomes "−12/s · runs out in 0:48" in danger red, and the `resources-value` outlines in danger. A single alert row is added the first time it happens ("Resources will run out in 1:00"), once per drop, repeating only after net has been positive.
- **D. Unpaid (decay active):** `net-value` "Decaying" in danger red, with the count of unpaid units and buildings ("12 decaying"). Clicking it selects them all.

The net readout never shows a sign flicker: it is the average of the last five seconds. Numbers are the same ones the server already sends for `income-value` and `upkeep-value`; no new network data.

## 5. Select all of a type, map-wide

**Frame `Selection panel – type chips`.** The type chips in the selection panel already keep only that type selected. Add:

- **Double-click a type chip** (or `Ctrl` + double-click on a unit on the map): select every own unit of that type **anywhere on the map**, not just on screen. The selection panel then shows a small "map-wide" tag next to its title, and the camera does not move.
- A chip that has more of its type on the map than are selected shows the total as a second figure ("12 / 31").

Unit-type selections cap at nothing (the box already selects without a limit).

## 6. Control groups for buildings

**Frame `Squad bar – building groups`.** The squad bar already shows ten slots with live unit counts. Slots may now hold **buildings**: `Ctrl` + number with only producers selected saves them, and a slot with buildings shows a small building icon instead of a unit count. A slot may hold units **or** buildings, not both (assigning across kinds replaces the slot). Pressing the number selects the producers and opens their command card; pressing it twice centres the camera on the first one. Producers lost to attack or demolition drop out of the slot. The slot hint ("Squads: Ctrl+number to save") reads "Groups" and mentions buildings too.

Nothing changes for units. A slot with buildings is drawn in the producer colour (the existing "Spawner" blue) so it is told apart at a glance.

## 8. Rally all producers, rally to a blueprint

**Frame `Rally – all producers`.**

- A **Rally all** command card button (`cmd-rally-all`, flag icon, key `Ctrl` + right-click) shown when any producer is selected: the next click sets the rally point of **all** your producers, not just the selected ones. The cursor tag reads "Rally all producers".
- **Rally onto a blueprint:** right-clicking a blueprint of your own as a rally point means newly made **Builders** go and help build it (other types just go near). The flag at the blueprint is drawn in the Builder colour and the tag reads "Builders will help build".
- The rally flag for a selected producer is drawn today; the flags for "all" are drawn together as a thin dashed spoke from each producer to the flag while `Ctrl` is held. Only you see them.

*Not designed:* rallying onto a unit to follow it. It needs a new order in the sim and is left out; say so if you want it.

## 10. Group-wide command feedback

**Frame `Order feedback – group`.** Orders today give feedback for the first selected unit only. New:

- **Move or Attack-move:** a ring at the goal sized to the group's gather radius, a dashed path from the selection's centre of mass (not the first unit), and a count tag ("12 units"). Attack-move rings are red with crossed-swords, Move rings the team colour.
- **Queued orders (Shift held):** numbered rings for the queue of **the selection** (the longest queue wins), not the first unit; a unit whose queue differs is not drawn separately.
- **A selection hull:** while a Move or Attack-move is armed, a thin outline around the selected units shows what will move.
- The feedback fades over 0.6 s after the click, as today.

Everything is computed on the client from the selection; no new network data.

## 11. Alert cycling, sounds and filters

**Frame `Alerts – cycle and filters`.**

- **Next alert (`Space`):** centres the camera on the newest alert that has a place and has not been visited, then the one before it on the next press. A small chip `next-alert` ("Space: next alert", count of unvisited alerts) sits at the top of the alert feed while any exist; clicking it does the same. Visited alerts dim slightly. New rebindable action `NextAlert`.
- **Alert sounds per category:** each alert kind has its own short sound (Under attack, Bomb triggered, Enemy spotted, Construction finished, Upkeep unpaid, Gift received, Resources will run out). Built through `WAR-2D/Audio/Build SFX` with the others; no new audio code beyond `AudioService` following the alert events.
- **Filters (Settings → Gameplay → Alerts):** one row per kind with two switches, **Show** and **Sound** (all on by default). Under attack and Upkeep unpaid can't be turned off together (the row's switches are disabled once both would be off), because losing those silently loses games.

## 12. Hover info for enemy and ghost buildings

**Frame `Hover card – building`.** Hovering a building (after 0.3 s) draws a card above the pointer:

- **Visible enemy building:** name, owner (name and colour chip), health bar and "300 / 500", and "Producing: …" only if it is already visible being made (no new information beyond what the client holds).
- **Ghost (last-seen):** name, owner, a "Last seen 2:14 ago" line in the secondary colour with the health **as last seen**, and a "Out of sight" tag. A ghost never shows "Producing".
- **Own building:** name, health, upkeep, and "Unpaid" in danger when it is.
- **Gem or terrain tiles:** no card.

The card uses the same visual as the command-tooltip (item 3), so one component is drawn.

Last-seen time is the client's own clock when it last saw the building (`ClientBuildings`); nothing new is sent, and `LeakTests` are unaffected.

## 13. Minimap filters and scale

**Frame `Minimap – filters`.**

- **Filter strip** along the minimap's bottom edge: four small toggles with icons: **Gems/resources**, **Enemy ghosts**, **Own army**, **Own buildings**. They only change what is drawn, never what is known. Defaults all on.
- **Scale:** three sizes (S 160 px, M 224 px, L 304 px at 1080p), a small `⤢` button cycles through them; the choice is saved with the settings. The command card and alert feed re-anchor to the minimap's edge, as they do today.
- At the large size on a 1024 map, the minimap draws the camera box with a 1 px outline so it stays visible.

## 14. Range preview for Sniper and Artillery

**Frame `Range rings – long range`.**

- **While selected**, a Sniper or Artillery shows its **maximum range** as the Radar's ring style (dashed, 2 px, 6 % fill) and its **minimum range** as a second smaller red ring with a hatch fill, so "can't fire inside 3 or 4 tiles" is visible.
- **While a Move or Attack-move is armed**, the ring follows the cursor so you can see what the unit would reach from there.
- **What it can fire at:** enemies **inside the ring that the team sees now** get a thin red bracket; enemies inside the ring that aren't seen get nothing (so no information leaks). A line in the selection panel reads "Needs a spotter: sight 7, range 14" when none is in sight.
- **Several selected:** rings for each *type* (not each unit): the largest and smallest of that type.

Rings are world overlays drawn by the existing Radar ring drawer, given an inner radius. No new data sent: range, minimum range and sight come from `GameConfig.xml`.

## 15 and 16. Settings → Gameplay (new tab)

**Frame `Settings – Gameplay`.** The new tab holds both the interface and camera settings, so the Controls tab stays for bindings. Rows, in order:

- **Interface**
  - **UI scale** (slider, 75 %–150 %, step 5 %): scales the whole HUD and menus; updates live. Default 100 %.
  - **Health bars:** segmented, **Always**, **Damaged only** (today's behaviour, default) or **Selected only**.
  - **Tracers:** segmented, **Full**, **Reduced** (one in three shots) or **Off**. A performance and clarity setting for big battles.
  - **Tooltips delay:** segmented, **Fast** (0.2 s), **Normal** (0.4 s, default), **Off**.
- **Camera** (these come from `Character_Settings.asset` today)
  - **Pan speed** (slider, 50 %–200 %), **Edge-scroll speed** (slider, 50 %–200 %; greyed when Edge scrolling is off), **Zoom speed** (slider), **Shift pan multiplier** (slider, 1.5×–4×). Each shows its value on the right and a Reset on the slider's end.
  - The Edge scrolling switch moves here from Controls (a link remains on the Controls page: "Edge scrolling: see Gameplay").
- **Alerts** (item 11): one row per kind with a Show and a Sound switch.
- **Confirmations** (new): switches for the two confirm dialogs that exist today, **Confirm demolish** and **Confirm surrender**, both on by default (the surrender confirm is also off-limits to switch off in a match with others, so it can't be lost by a misclick: the switch is disabled with a hint when the host sets Teams).

**Reset to defaults** on the screen covers the new tab too. UI scale and camera speeds apply at once and are saved in `settings.json` (`GameSettings`).

## 17. Remove the dead settings

**Frame `Settings – Graphics`.** The Graphics page without the **Save replays** row (`save-replays-slot`). Nothing reads `GameSettings.SaveReplays` any more (the host always records, and only the host keeps a copy), so the switch does nothing. The `Replays/SendKilobytesPerSecond` config key goes with it (parser, `GameConfig`, XML, tests). Existing `settings.json` files that still contain `SaveReplays` are read and ignored. The rest of the page is unchanged.

## 19. Reconnect after a drop

**Frame `Reconnect`.** Today a player who drops mid-match has their units and buildings destroyed. New: they get a **grace period** (`Match/ReconnectSeconds`, 60 s, host-configurable later) to come back.

- **The dropped player (when they return to the main menu):** a **Rejoin** button on the Play screen's top row, with the host's name and time left ("Rejoin Cmdr. Vasquez's match · 0:42"). Pressing it reconnects and resumes with the world they left. After the grace period the button disappears and the player is out.
- **The others in the match:** a banner `reconnect-banner` under the top bar ("Cmdr. Vasquez disconnected · 0:42 to return"), and an alert row ("Player disconnected"/"Player returned"). The dropped player's units **hold position and don't produce** while they are away; their upkeep stops, so nothing decays. If the countdown reaches zero they are eliminated exactly as today.
- **The host leaves:** unchanged (match ends); this is not host migration.
- **Not shown:** AIs never disconnect.

*Cost and risk:* this touches the server's join rules (`LobbyRules.MayJoin`), identity (Steam id; Direct has none, so Direct rejoin needs a token the server hands out on first join), the `ServerPlayer`/`ClientPlayer` lifetimes, and a full snapshot resend for the returning client (units, buildings, fog, terrain). `LeakTests` must prove the returning player learns only their own team's world. It is by far the largest item here: see decisions.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Idle Builder keys.** `.` Idle Builders, `Ctrl+.` All Builders, `,` Army, `Backspace` HQ, `Space` next alert, `Ctrl`+right-click rally all. Alternatives welcome; all are rebindable. (`,` and `.` are the common RTS pair for these.)
2. **Army excludes Diggers and bombers** until pressed twice. Or: army = everything but Builders.
3. **Mixed slots:** a squad slot holds units **or** buildings, not both. Alternative: allow both and have the number select both (more powerful, but a command card for a mixed selection is already complicated).
4. **Net income is the five-second average.** Alternative: the instantaneous value (jumpier).
5. **Reconnect grace period: 60 s**, units hold and don't produce while away. Alternatives: 120 s; or let the dropped player's units keep fighting on defence (harder to balance). **Direct (IP) rejoin** needs a token the host hands out; it is not available when a client was never authenticated. Do you want Direct rejoin, or Steam only?
6. **Reconnect scope.** It is a networking feature with its own risks (join rules, snapshot resend, leak proofs). Recommended: build it **last**, behind its own branch and plan, and ship the other fifteen first. The frame is drawn here so you can approve the UX now.
7. **Rally to a unit (follow)** is left out (it needs a sim change). Say if you want it.
8. **Surrender confirm** can't be switched off in Teams matches with others, to prevent a misclick losing a game; the demolish confirm can be.
9. **Settings tab name:** Gameplay (recommended), or Interface. And whether to move Edge scrolling off the Controls tab.
10. **Tracers Reduced/Off** is a visual-only setting; it changes nothing the server sends. OK as written?
11. **Alerts can't all be turned off:** Under attack and Upkeep unpaid keep at least one of Show or Sound.
12. **Icons.** Six new 16×16 icons are drafted with the frames; a rejected one gets a round of three options, as before.

### Deviations from the spec's layout

1. **Quick-select (1) moves to the top bar**, right of the resources block, as three live counts: idle Builders, all Builders and Army. The spec put it left of the squad bar, and the full-screen finding put it above the minimap. Both crowd the bottom left, which the owner rejected. The top bar has room, and these counts read like a supply counter. All Builders shows the plain Builder icon and its total, without the "∞" mark.
2. **Net income (4) becomes the main rate readout.** It sits next to the balance in the state colour, and `income-value` and `upkeep-value` drop to a quieter secondary breakdown. State B adds a ▼ and a tooltip. State D reads "12 decaying · select".
3. **The command hover card (3)** opens to the left of the command card, as in the earlier finding. Its detail line becomes label and value pairs (Cost 150 · Upkeep 4/s · Size 1×1 · Range 7), with the cost in gold, or in red when it is the reason the button is disabled.
4. **Command buttons are drawn 56×56 with the shipped 32 px pixel icons**, as `CommandCard.uss` draws them, using a new local component. The Figma `Command button` set (52 px vector icons) no longer matches the game.
5. **A building group slot (6)** shows the producer's icon and count. It does not use the producer blue (spec) or the gold outline (earlier finding), because both clash with existing meanings (player 1's colour, and the readout colour). A selected building slot uses the accent outline, like a unit slot.
6. **The map-wide tag (5)** is an accent-outlined "Map-wide" chip in the selection title.
7. **Rally all (8) lives in the production card's existing "Rally point" row**, as a command button labelled `Ctrl RMB`, rather than as a new button in the general command rows. The cursor tag adds the producer count: "Rally all producers (4)".
8. **The minimap filters (13) open from an eye button** in the minimap title bar as a popover of labelled switches. Each switch has the swatch that the minimap draws for that layer. The eye outlines gold while any filter is off. Size is an S/M/L segmented control in the title bar instead of a ⤢ button that cycles. The spec's strip along the bottom edge would grow the panel.
9. **Settings › Gameplay (15, 16, 11) uses two columns**: Interface, Camera and Confirmations on the left, the alert Show/Sound table on the right. The tab then fits 1080p without scrolling. The Settings panel is 1000 px wide. A slider's Reset appears only when its value differs from the default, and sits left of the slider so the right edges stay aligned.
10. **Range rings (14) carry small labels** ("range 14", "min 4"). The selection-panel hint "Needs a spotter: sight 7, range 14" is drawn in the warning colour.
11. **Reconnect (19).** The banner also says what the dropped army does and drains a countdown bar. The others' top bar greys the name and adds the reconnect icon and timer. Banners for "returned" (green) and "did not return" (danger) close after 4 s. On the Play screen, Rejoin is a highlighted card on the first row (host, time left, a Rejoin button), with a "Rejoining…" state that has Cancel, and a danger state under 10 s.
12. **The diplomacy panel is closed in the mock-ups.** It is a toggle panel and unchanged by this batch.

Behaviour, the "Decisions for the owner" list and the reconnect scope are unchanged.

### Known limits of the drawing

- The minimap texture is still the placeholder from the approved v0.14 frame.
- The sprites are downscaled smoothly by Figma, so they are not pixel-exact.
- The fog in B is a stepped overlay, not the game's fog shader.
- The Toggle and Button instances are the older v0.6 components (rounded switches), with their labels set to Jersey 10.
- Figma can't bind a page background to a variable, so the page background is the Theme base value (#26303A) as a plain colour.

# v0.16 Clarity (Figma brief)

The HUD and menu work in v0.16 ([spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-v0.16-clarity-design.md)), to be drawn before anything is built. Same look as v0.6 to v0.15.1 (Jersey 10, square corners, the `Theme` and `Scale` variables, the existing `Command button (pixel, 56)`, `Key chip`, `Button` components and `Icon/px/*` icons).

- **File:** [WAR-2D v0.6 UX](https://www.figma.com/design/JrMColnJiQbFfODqyPjBYf), a new page **v0.16 Clarity**.
- **Status:** brief written 2026-11-01; **frames not drawn yet**. Nothing in v0.16's HUD or menus is built until the owner approves the frames.

## Frames to draw

| Frame | What it shows |
| ----- | ------------- |
| T · Target lines | A selected group of mixed units in a fight: lines to a unit, a building and a wall target (red, red, orange), a dashed attack-move goal line, at close and medium zoom. |
| S1 · Strategic icons | One 8×8 icon per unit type (Builder, Tank, Digger, Scout, Heavy, Artillery, Sniper, Flamethrower, Suicide bomber) and one symbol per building type, in two team colours and as a ghost. These go into `Art/PixelIcons.cs` as drawn. |
| S2 · Strategic zoom | A 1024 map zoomed out: armies as icons, bases as symbols, fog, a selection, before and after the threshold. |
| C · Minimap combat | The minimap with three pulses (one involving the player's units in the alert colour, two others white), at the start, middle and end of a pulse. |
| P · HQ-placement minimap | The minimap during _PlacingHQ_ on 256, 512 and 1024: the whole terrain readable, the player's options numbered to match the Option buttons, a teammate's options smaller in their colour, the camera box. |
| G · Settings | Gameplay rows: Target lines (Selected, Off), Strategic zoom (On, Off), Formations (On, Off). Graphics row: Reduce motion (Off, On). |
| M1 · Menu motion | The main menu, Play, Lobby, Settings and Replays screens with the transition between each pair noted (direction, duration, easing); a dialog scaling in; button hover, pressed and disabled states. |
| M2 · Lobby | The lobby with player cards (colour, name, team, ready, the AI tag with difficulty and style), the larger map preview with size and seed, the settings grouped into Map, Start and Rules; with 2, 5 and 8 players; as host and as a joined player. |

## Notes for the frames

- Target lines and the strategic icons must read at 1080p and at 4K (the HUD `Scale` variable), and stay 1 px or crisp pixel multiples.
- Strategic icons are recognisable by shape alone (the colour is the owner's), and differ from the command card icons enough not to be mistaken for buttons.
- The HQ-placement markers carry the same numbers as the Option buttons on the HUD overlay, and must not hide the terrain around the option (an outline ring with the number beside it, not a filled disc).
- Motion stays quick (150–250 ms) and never delays input: a click during a transition goes to the screen being shown.

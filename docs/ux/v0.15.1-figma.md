## H1–H3. Card-slot hotkeys

The keys belong to card **slots**, not to buildings, so one set of keys serves the Builders' card and the producers' card:

- **Top row** `CardTop1`–`CardTop4`: `Q` `W` `E` `T`. Builders: Miner, Barracks, Heavy Factory, Siege Workshop. Producers: the unit types in their button order (HQ: Builder; Barracks: Scout, Tank, Flamethrower; Heavy Factory: Heavy, Artillery, Sniper; Siege Workshop: Digger, Suicide bomber).
- **Bottom row** `CardBottom1`–`CardBottom5`: `Z` `X` `C` `V` `N`. Builders: Wall, Bomb, Radar, Gun turret, Jammer.
- `R` (Rotate while placing) and `B` (Detonate) are skipped, so no default key does two things (`GameInputTests.CardSlotKeysClashWithNoOtherDefault`).
- A build key does what its button does (place, or arm the Wall drag); while placing, another key switches building. A key on a greyed-out button places nothing and the cursor tag says why for 1 s ("Radar: need 250 (you have 90)").
- A production key on the type being made queues one more at every selected producer (`Shift`: ten); on another type it switches to it, as a click does. "Queue full" when no producer has room. Mixed producers: nothing.
- Keys are ignored while the in-match menu, Settings, Gift or Diplomacy is open, on the end screen and while spectating.
- The key shows top-left on each button (`command-button__hotkey`) and in its hover card; a rebind shows when Settings closes.

Element names: `build-*-key` (UXML), `type-key` (production buttons, built in code). Actions: `CardTop1`–`CardTop4`, `CardBottom1`–`CardBottom5`.

## R1. Reconnect states

- **Left the match** (`ReconnectBannerKind.Left`): a player who disappears without being away first left on purpose or was removed. Danger stripe, no countdown, "Their units and buildings are removed.", closes after 4 s.
- **Refused rejoin**: the server answers a dead ticket with `RejoinRefused{Reason}`. The Play screen card turns danger, reads "You can’t rejoin this match", the tail gives the reason (eliminated while away, time ran out, removed for flooding commands, left the match, the match is over, unknown ticket), the countdown bar is hidden and only **Dismiss** shows.
- **Leaving…**: Leave match keeps the in-match menu open, greys every button and reads "Leaving…" while the client waits (at most 1 s) for the server to close the connection after `LeaveMatch`.

## P. Proposals (awaiting approval)

1. **Keys sheet:** a "Keys" button in the in-match menu (and `F1`) opening a read-only list of every current binding, grouped Orders / Card / Camera / Selection.
2. **Producer idle alert:** "Barracks finished its queue" when an own producer's queue runs out (click centres on it), with a row in Settings › Gameplay › Alerts, off by default.
3. **Production progress:** a 3 px bar along the bottom of the active type button with the next unit's build progress. Needs a new owner-only progress update; today the client only knows the queue count.
4. **One Shift:** merge "Queue order" and the fixed box-select append into one rebindable "Queue / add to selection" row, closing the open `bug-review.md` item.
5. **Away countdown in the player list:** the top bar's away tag turns danger under 10 s, matching the banner.

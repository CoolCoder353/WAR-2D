# v0.17 Heartland: rich gems in the HUD (Figma brief)

The only HUD in v0.17 (spec §4): what a Miner says about the gem it mines, and how rich gems read on the minimap. Same look as v0.16 ([v0.16-figma.md](v0.16-figma.md)): Jersey 10, square corners, the `Theme` variables; the hover card is the v0.15 building hover card (frame 12).

## The colour (owner's pick)

Gems are red in the game's palette (`Gem1` #C62828). A tile's flat and minimap colour must be one of the palette's colours (`TileAutotileTests.FlatTileColoursComeFromThePalette`), so:

- **A (proposed): the palette's gold, `Warn` #F2C14E.** Brighter and warmer than the red gems, and clear on the minimap. (A first draft mixed `Gem3` with `Warn`, #F8C283; it isn't a palette colour and read close to white on the minimap.)
- **B: an orange, #F1894C.** Closer to the red of ordinary gems; not a palette colour, so the palette would need a new role for it.

The tile art (spec §4) tints its crystals from `Gem3` to `Warn` (shading inside the art may blend palette colours; only flat colours must be exact).

## Notes for building (after approval)

- `BuildingHover.MiningLine(TileType faced, float miningRate, float richMultiplier)` gives the footer text; `BuildingHoverCard.Fill` shows it for own finished Miners.
- The placement label is drawn by `BuildingPlacement` while the Miner is the building being placed, from `MinerRules.RateOf`.
- `MapView.TileColor(TileType.RichGem)` holds the chosen colour (one line); the minimap and the tile art read it.

# v0.17 Heartland: rich gems in the HUD (Figma brief)

The only HUD in v0.17 (spec §4): what a Miner says about the gem it mines, and how rich gems read on the minimap. Same look as v0.16 ([v0.16-figma.md](v0.16-figma.md)): Jersey 10, square corners, the `Theme` variables; the hover card is the v0.15 building hover card (frame 12).

- **File:** [WAR-2D v0.6 UX](https://www.figma.com/design/JrMColnJiQbFfODqyPjBYf), page **v0.17 Heartland** (node 183:2).
- **Status:** drawn 2026-11-03, **awaiting the owner's approval**. Nothing here is built until then.

## Frames

| Frame | Node | What it shows |
| ----- | ---- | ------------- |
| H1 · Rich gems: the Miner's lines and the minimap | 183:6 | (a) An own finished Miner's hover card gains a footer line: "Mining rich gem: 25/s", or "Mining: 10/s" facing an ordinary gem (a Miner has no upkeep, so its footer was empty; construction and the unpaid line keep priority; not on enemy Miners). (b) While a Miner is placed, a small label under the green ghost says what the faced tile pays: "Rich gem: 25/s" or "Gem: 10/s"; none when it faces neither (the ghost is red then). (c) The minimap draws rich gem cells in their own colour; the Gems layer shows or hides both kinds and its legend swatch shows both. |

## The colour (owner's pick)

Gems are red in the game's palette (`Gem1` #C62828). Two candidates for rich gems, both from the palette's own roles:

- **A (proposed): `Gem3` halfway to `Warn`, #F8C283, pale gold.** Reads as treasure; on the minimap it is light and can look close to white at small sizes.
- **B: `Gem2` halfway to `Warn`, #F1894C, orange.** Stands out more on the minimap; closer to the red of ordinary gems.

The tile art (spec §4) uses the same colour for its crystals.

## Notes for building (after approval)

- `BuildingHover.MiningLine(TileType faced, float miningRate, float richMultiplier)` gives the footer text; `BuildingHoverCard.Fill` shows it for own finished Miners.
- The placement label is drawn by `BuildingPlacement` while the Miner is the building being placed, from `MinerRules.RateOf`.
- `MapView.TileColor(TileType.RichGem)` holds the chosen colour (one line); the minimap and the tile art read it.

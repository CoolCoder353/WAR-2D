# v0.20 Onslaught (Figma brief)

The HUD and manual work in v0.20 ([spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-v0.20-onslaught-design.md)), to be drawn before any of it is built. A small round (owner, 2026-11-06). Same look as v0.6 to v0.18 (Jersey 10, square corners, the `Theme` and `Scale` variables, the existing `Command button (pixel, 56)`, `Key chip`, `Button` components and `Icon/px/*` icons).

- **File:** [WAR-2D v0.6 UX](https://www.figma.com/design/JrMColnJiQbFfODqyPjBYf), a new page **v0.20 Onslaught**.
- **Status:** not drawn.
- **In-world art is not in Figma.** The new sprites (the 2×2 Sniper and Gun turret with their turning heads), the leap, fire, laser, shells and deaths, and the sounds are generated, and the owner approves them on the asset review page (spec §8.4); the checklist is [art/v0.20-asset-review.md](../art/v0.20-asset-review.md), which also tracks these frames.
- **The manual's words** for the new rules are drafted in `tools/manual-text.json`, regenerated as the mock website (`node tools/manual-mock.js`) and approved there before B3 is drawn, as in v0.18.

## Frames

| Frame | What it shows |
| ----- | ------------- |
| B1 · Status on the battlefield and the selection | A burning marker (a small flame) on a unit's and a building's world health bar, and on the selection panel's per-type button when any of that type burns; a thin reload bar under the health bar of a selected Artillery and Sniper, and the Sniper's bar as an aim bar while it aims; an airborne Bomber's health bar hidden. At close and medium zoom, in the Standard and Colourblind palettes. |
| B2 · Hover cards and the command card | The unit hover card's new lines where they apply (Blast resistance, Armour, Sets on fire, Aim time, Reload), for the Heavy, Sniper, Flamethrower and Artillery; the Siege Workshop's production row with the **Bomber** (renamed, its icon) and the Builders' build row with the **Mine** (renamed, its icon) and its key chip; the **Gun turret** button's hover card with its new cost and build time, and its 2×2 placement preview with its range ring; the Detonate button unchanged. |
| B3 · Field manual | The Bomber and Sniper pages with the new stat block lines, and the Rules section's Combat page with blast resistance, armour, fire and spotting (one small diagram each: a Scout's sight letting Artillery fire beyond its own). Drawn from the approved mock. |

## Notes for the frames

- The burning marker and the reload bar must stay readable at UI scale 0.75 and not hide the health bar's value; colour is never the only cue (the flame has a shape).
- Nothing on these frames shows what a player doesn't have: burning and aiming show only on units and buildings the player is sent, and an enemy's reload bar is never shown.
- The renamed icons keep the old silhouettes' weight so the command card's rhythm doesn't change.

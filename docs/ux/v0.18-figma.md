The HUD and menu work in v0.18 ([spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-v0.18-briefing-design.md)), to be drawn before anything is built. ## Notes for the frames

- The manual must stay readable at UI scale 0.75 and at 4K, and fit 1280×720 without the stat block scrolling sideways.
- Graph colours are the player colours of the chosen palette (Standard, Colourblind, High contrast); lines also differ by the local player's thickness, and the legend carries names, so colour is never the only cue.
- Rings stay thin and quiet: they show while a key is held, so they may be clearer than target lines, but must not hide health bars.
- Nothing on these frames shows information a player doesn't have: the manual and rings come from the config, the graphs and timeline only appear at the end.

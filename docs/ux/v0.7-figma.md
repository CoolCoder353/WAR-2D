## Decisions for the owner

1. **Keys.** The plan said Repair on R, but R is already Rotate (building placement). The draft has **Repair F, Dig G, Cancel/Demolish Delete**. Build buttons keep no keys, as in v0.6.
2. **Costs on build buttons.** The cost takes the key-hint slot under each build icon (gold readout). The alternative is a tooltip only.
3. **Demolish confirm.** Inline in the command card ("Demolish Spawner? You get 100 back." with Demolish / Keep). Delete pressed twice also confirms, Esc backs out. Cancel on a blueprint has no confirm, since its refund is full.
4. **New icons.** Bomb (a half-sunk disc with a red fuse light), Repair (a wrench), Demolish (a building with a red crack).
5. **Production rows.** One row per unit type, with the per-unit cost. This also fixes the known issue that Diggers can't be queued from the HUD.
6. **Alert texts.** "Enemy spotted by Radar", "Bomb triggered", "Construction finished: <building>". A fourth row, "Out of resources: repairs stopped", is proposed (repairs stop when broke, plan P4); drop it if unwanted.
7. **Minimap pings.** Red ring for a Radar contact, drawn at the Radar's tile (the ping must not reveal where the enemy is); orange for a bomb. Both pulse twice, then fade.
8. **World look.** Blueprints at 25 % team tint with a dashed outline, the tint filling in with progress, and a gold progress bar under the footprint. Walls get team bands and damage frames at < 66 % and < 33 % health. The drag preview is team-tinted where valid and red where blocked, with the readout "N tiles · cost" and "M blocked". The Radar ring is dashed in team colour. A bomb's blast radius shows only while placing or hovering.

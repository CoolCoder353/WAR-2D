# v0.20 Onslaught: asset review

Every new or changed asset in v0.20 is approved by the owner before it lands ([spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-v0.20-onslaught-design.md) §8.4). This file is the checklist. v0.20 doesn't merge while any row is not **approved**.

- **How a batch is reviewed:** the assets are generated (`RenderGen`, `EffectGen`, the sound JSON in `Assets/Art/Audio/sfx/`) and gathered on one review page, a private artifact link rebuilt for each batch. Sprites are shown in every player colour and facing at 1×, 2× and 4× zoom, effects as frame strips and short clips from a real match (through the control API), and sounds as players. The owner approves or comments there or in conversation.
- **States:** not drawn → awaiting approval (with the review page's link) → approved (with the date), or changes asked (with what to change).
- **HUD and manual** rows are approved in Figma ([brief](../ux/v0.20-figma.md)) and the manual's mock, and are listed here so one place shows everything.
- **Review page (2026-11-06):** https://claude.ai/artifact/66s8CUNNdgG3T1KFcXc4wt, built from `docs/art/v0.20/index.html` and the renders of `WAR-2D/Art/Render v0.20 samples`. Batches 2 and 3 are shown as clips staged from the generators on a bare floor; clips from a real match replace them once the rules are built. **Every row below was approved on 2026-11-06.** The generators are committed (`RenderGen.Onslaught`, `EffectGen.Onslaught`, `Sfxr`'s repeat option); the renders, clips and sound set are in `docs/art/v0.20/`. Still to do when the update starts: register the sprites and effects in `ArtManifest` and `Editor/ArtBuild.cs` and run `WAR-2D/Art/Build all`, move the sounds from `docs/art/v0.20/sfx-src/` to `Assets/Art/Audio/sfx/` and run `WAR-2D/Audio/Build SFX`, snap the larger shell's soft edges and one armour-spark shade to the effect palette, and replace the staged clips with ones from a real match. The old sounds in `docs/art/v0.20/sfx/old_*` are the A/B reference only.
- **Order:** batch 1 first (the Sniper's and Gun turret's heads are needed before the aim and turret work is checked), then each rework's effects as its rules are built, then the sounds.

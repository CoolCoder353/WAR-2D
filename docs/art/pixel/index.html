WAR-2D pixel style samples (round 4)

The lit models from round 2, finished as pixel art: 32 px per tile (your current art's size), every pixel snapped to a limited Charcoal palette with no blending, a 1 px dark outline and hard 2 px shadows. Blue and red are player colours 1 and 2.

Round 4: tanks and terrain are unchanged (approved). The buildings were redesigned with their own silhouettes; their footprint is still square for gameplay, but the ground shows around them:

  • Miner: a small tracked rig with a team-coloured cab and an ore hopper; its boom reaches east to a big spiral auger at the gem face, with ore spilling beside it. The auger turns when animated.
  • Small Unit Spawner: a factory hall with a sawtooth roof (lit slopes and skylight strips), a team stripe, a roller door onto a striped loading apron, an office annex with a team roof plate, a tall smokestack, and fuel tanks piped to the annex.
  • HQ: a compound: a cross-shaped command building with the team chevron and AC units, a lattice comms tower with a dish and beacon, a round helipad, a garage with a striped apron, walkways, crates and fuel drums.

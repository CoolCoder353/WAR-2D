Wave Fix Sounds

The build read every sound spec's wave as Square, whatever the file said, so these 35 sounds shipped as square waves. Before is what ships today. After is the same spec rendered with the wave it asks for (Noise, Saw or Sine). The specs were written while the bug hid their real sound, so some of the after sounds may need work.

Each sound shows its waveform and its spectrogram (0 to 11 kHz, bottom to top, brighter is louder). Pick one answer per sound; your answers are kept in this browser. When done, copy the summary at the end and paste it back to Claude.

# Playtest 2026-11-10 (main @ f1b04d56)

A fresh Linux Development build of `main`, played through the control API (`tools/war2d`) as a host plus a joined client over Direct (KCP) at 1920×1080. Six matches: an 8-player 512 FFA with Hard AIs, three 256 matches (FFA vs Normal AI, two humans alone, 2v2 Teams vs Easy AIs), a mid-match leave and rejoin, and an 8-player 1024 FFA. Also a 20-match AI bench (`tools/ai-bench.sh --brief 2 3 Builds/Dev/WAR-2D.x86_64`, 256 and 512) and `node tools/balance-sheet.js`.

**101 issues**, none of them already listed in `known-issues.md` or `bug-review.md` (items that were are left out; the four that are close to a listed one say so). Grouped by area, and ranked within each area by importance and impact (1 = fix first). Each area starts with its worst issue.

Severity tags: **High** breaks a match or misleads the player about something that matters; **Med** visibly wrong or costly but survivable; **Low** polish.

Areas are ordered by how much their worst issues hurt a real match: Server, Balance, Networking, UI, Units, Buildings, Other.

## Top 10 across all areas

1. Server 1: an empty bank kills units even when income covers upkeep.
2. Balance 1–2: the rich-gem economy runs away, and the AI banks millions it can't spend.
3. Balance 3–5: Easy, Normal and Hard all end a new player's match in about 2–3 minutes.
4. Server 2: AI armies keep chasing unreachable targets, so matches stall at the 40-minute cap.
5. Networking 1: the client throws a NullReferenceException every frame after leaving a match.
6. Server 3: a start site with no gem vein, which stalls whoever starts there.
7. Networking 2: the host leaving shows the client a Rejoin banner for a match that no longer exists.
8. Units 1: idle Builders auto-work anywhere on the map (900-tile radius) and wander into enemy ground.
9. UI 1–2: the squad bar hidden under the selection panel at 1080p, and the opaque in-match Settings in a running multiplayer match.
10. UI 3–4: the 8-player spectator bar runs off the screen, and the client lobby shows Diplomacy as off when it's on.

## Server (simulation, economy, AI decisions, map generation)

1. **High.** A momentarily empty bank kills units even when income covers their upkeep. With 30 Tanks (15/s upkeep) and +22/s income, setting resources to 0 killed 14 Tanks within about 2 s. Upkeep is charged once a second from whatever is in the bank (`SimEconomySystem` `UpkeepJob`); every unit charged at 0 is unpaid for the whole second, and each unpaid unit costs a whole unit. Spending down to 0 on production just before the charge costs a chunk of the army. (Related to the known "long production queue bankrupts you"; this is the mechanism and shows it also happens with positive net income.)
2. **High.** AI armies keep re-targeting buildings they can't reach. Hard Expander logged "gave up on 194,259: not reached in 240 s" at 26.6 and 34.6 min and picked 194,259 again at 30.6 and 38.6 min; its rival marched 427 units "0 there" for four minutes, gave up on 82,370, then picked 82,370 again at 18.9 and 33.5 min. Both 512 Expander mirrors (Hard-Hard, Normal-Normal) hit the 40-minute cap. One cause behind the known cap count.
3. **High.** Map generation left a start site without a gem vein (1024, seed 7, 8 players, Random pick): the nearest gems to the HQ at (817,767) are 47–60 tiles away. Every start site is meant to get a guaranteed vein. The Hard AI there sat for over 5 minutes with 7 idle Builders, no Miners and about 7/s income.
4. **Med.** An AI whose only request is a Miner it can't place ("Miner(full)") never falls back to Diggers, an expansion or an army. It built one Gun turret and stalled with 3,800 banked.
5. **Med.** A stalled AI is never conceded or flagged: the 8-player FFA kept a dead seat (7 Builders at 5:15) that no one would find until they walked over.
6. **Med.** Hard AI keeps 9–10 idle Builders while its own request list wants Miners (bench timeline "idle 9 req 2 rq [Miner,Miner]" at 30 and 36 min).
7. **Med.** HQ destruction and player elimination aren't logged at all (the host log had no line when player 2 was eliminated), so nobody can tell afterwards who killed an HQ or how.

## Balance

1. **High.** The rich-gem economy runs away. A Hard AI at 145 s had 35 Miners, 5,424/s income and 262k banked on a 512 map; in the bench a Hard Expander peaked at 44.6 M resources (41,595/s at 36 min). 375/s per rich Miner dwarfs every cost, so money means nothing after about 6 minutes.
2. **High.** The AI can't spend what it earns: IronFox sat at "banked, 257k free, Defend, attacks 0"; bench mean highest balance was 9.3 M for Hard and 8.1 M for Normal. Matches the balance sheet's flag (IncomePerProducer 90/s against at most 50/s a producer can spend).
3. **High.** Easy isn't a beginner opponent: an Easy Rusher eliminated a human with 16 Tanks at 2:44 in a 2v2 on 256 with 68 units; its Turtle teammate had 116 units.
4. **High.** Normal AI (Balanced) on 256 had 168 units and had mined 22,470 by 1:32, and had eliminated two players by about 2:00.
5. **High.** Hard AI rushed an idle player with 74 Tanks and Flamethrowers and killed the HQ about 2.5 minutes into an 8-player 512 match on 2,500 starting resources.
6. **Med.** The bench didn't meet its exit criteria: 3 of 20 matches (15 %) hit the 40-minute cap, all on 512.
7. **Med.** 256 matches end absurdly fast: in the bench Hard Rusher beat Normal Rusher in 1.1 min, and Normal Expander beat Easy in 1.6 min.
8. **Med.** Styles are lopsided: the stronger side won 8 of 8 as Rusher or Expander, while in mirror matches Turtle and Balanced won only 1 of 3 each. At 10 min Easy already banks 788,599 with 2,350/s income.
9. **Med.** One Artillery (range 36) kills a Gun turret (range 9) unharmed in 75 s, and one Sniper (28) in 175 s. The turret has no answer to being outranged (balance sheet).
10. **Med.** Digger and Sniper each win only 1 of 6 equal-cost matchups (the Digger loses even to Scouts, −98).
11. **Med.** Late AI armies skew to Bombers and Artillery: 274 Bombers and 291 Artillery in a 1,008 Hard army; 251 Bombers in a 613 Normal army.
12. **Med.** Easy Turtle over-builds Diggers (39 of 116 units at 2:44).
13. **Low.** Normal AI attacks into repeated bad trades: a 0.17 trade at 30.7 min, then it attacks again at 32.4 min with "advantage 61" and loses 0.41. The advantage figure doesn't predict fights.
14. **Low.** gameplay.md says unpaid upkeep costs "one unit a second". The code and the Field manual make it one unit per unpaid unit per second (14 units in one second in Server 1).

## Networking

1. **High.** After "Leave match" and a second return to the main menu, the client throws a NullReferenceException every frame (2,337 in its log), from `MenuBattle.LateUpdate` → `ClientWorld.Draw` (`ClientWorld.cs:525`, `store` is null). The menu's background battle stops.
2. **High.** When the host shuts down mid-match, the client is dropped to the Play screen with no "host left" message. Instead it shows "Your match is still running · Rejoin" with a countdown for a match that no longer exists.
3. **Med.** Pressing that Rejoin says "Couldn't get back into the match. Try again while the timer runs.", which invites pointless retries and gives no reason.
4. **Med.** A client's "Leave match" disconnects at once with no confirmation and gives up the seat for good (Surrender does ask). The host's case is already known; this is the client's.
5. **Med.** After leaving a running match the player lands on the main menu, which says nothing about rejoining. The Rejoin banner only appears one screen deeper, on Play.
6. **Med.** There is no text chat anywhere (lobby, match, spectating, end screen); pings are the only way to talk to other players.

# v0.13 Bastion: Design

**Status:** **approved by the owner on 2026-10-16**, with one change: the Gun turret shoots everything (units, buildings and walls), like a unit that can't move. Numbers remain starting values to tune in play. Not started; the implementation plan is written when v0.13 starts.
**Parent:** [roadmap](../../roadmap.md) §2 v0.13.

## 1. Goal

Two static defences that reward holding ground, built by Builders from blueprints like every other building:

1. the **Gun turret**: the game's first building that attacks;
2. the **Jammer**: an area where enemies can only see up close and enemy Radars see nothing.

**Out of scope:** other turret kinds, gates, tank traps, shields, decoys (all listed for later in the roadmap).

## 2. Gun turret

### 2.1 Rules

- 1×1, so it fits a chokepoint. Health 350, cost 150, upkeep 4, sight 8 (proposals).
- **Attacks everything, like a static unit** (owner, 2026-10-16): damage 8 every 0.4 s at range 7, with the v0.12 rules (a clear line of fire, the target's cell visible on its owner's grid). It picks targets in the same order a unit on attack-move does: the nearest enemy unit, else the nearest enemy building, else the nearest enemy wall tile. So it defends against units first, and still chips at a wall or building pushed up next to it.
- Damage-table row (attacker `GunTurret`): LightUnit 1.5, HeavyUnit 0.4, Building 0.5, Wall 0.5. Strong against Scouts, Flamethrowers and bombers, slow against buildings and walls, weak against Heavies and outranged by Artillery (14) and Snipers (16).
- It doesn't fire while a blueprint or under construction, and stops when its upkeep is unpaid (it decays like any building).

### 2.2 Implementation

- `<Building>` gains optional weapon fields (`Damage`, `Range`, `AttackInterval`); a building with `Damage > 0` is an attacker.
- The damage table gains rows for attacker building types after the unit types (`<Entry attacker="GunTurret" target="LightUnit">`), so `DamageTable.Get` takes an attacker index that is either a unit type or `UnitTypeCount + buildingType`.
- `SimCombatSystem` gains a **building attack job**: for each attacker building slot (a few hundred at most), the same target search units use (`TargetSearch`: enemy units over the unit spatial hash, then enemy buildings over the building hash, then enemy wall tiles; line of fire, fog-gated, sliced over ticks), cooldown, and a `DamageHit` into the same queue (wall hits into the wall-damage queue), so `ApplyDamageJob` stays the single writer. Reusing `TargetSearch` rather than writing a second one keeps the turret's rules identical to a unit's. Building weapon state (target, cooldown) lives in new `SimData` building arrays.
- **Attack events** gain an attacker kind (unit or building). The encoder sends a building's attack to a client only when that client may know the building (it owns it or, its grid sees it), the same rule `BuildingInterest` follows, and names the target by the existing `MayKnowTarget` rule. Clients draw the tracer from the turret's position in `ClientBuildings` and turn its head toward the target.
- **Art:** a base and a head with 8 facings (`RenderGen`), a muzzle flash (`EffectGen`), a shot SFX.

## 3. Jammer

### 3.1 Rules

- 2×2, health 200, cost 200, upkeep 6, radius 12 (proposals).
- While complete and paid, its radius is **jammed** for every owner who is not the Jammer's owner and whom the owner doesn't share vision with:
  - such an owner's units and buildings see a jammed fog cell only from within `JamSight` (3 tiles) of it, line of sight still required;
  - their **Radars see nothing** in jammed cells, and a Radar never pings for a contact in one.
- The jam is an area effect: it limits sight of everything in the area, not only the Jammer owner's things (an enemy also sees a third player's units there only up close). The Jammer itself is inside its radius, so enemies see it only up close too (a last-seen ghost stays a ghost).
- Allies (owners the Jammer's owner shares vision with) are unaffected. A defeated player spectating still sees everything.

### 3.2 Implementation

- `SimVisionSystem` keeps a **jam mask** per grid (one byte per fog cell, per owner slot), rebuilt only when a Jammer finishes, dies or loses upkeep, or when share-vision changes, not every vision pass.
- When a source stamps grid *g*, a jammed cell of *g* is marked visible only if the source is within `JamSight`. Radar stamps (`StampRadarJob`) skip jammed cells. `RadarService` ignores contacts on jammed cells.
- Because fog interest, building interest, terrain deltas and explosions all follow the grid, hiding works everywhere at once: nothing about a jammed unit reaches an enemy client beyond `JamSight`.
- **Art:** a building with a dish and a pulse effect; the owner sees its jam radius as a ring while it's selected (like the Radar's range ring). SFX for completion.

## 4. HUD (Figma first)

Build buttons for the Gun turret and the Jammer in the Builder's menu, their icons, the Jammer's range ring, and alert rows: "Turret under attack" uses the existing Under-attack alert. Nothing is built before the owner approves the frames.

## 6. Testing and performance

- **EditMode:** a turret fires at an enemy unit, building and wall in range, line and sight, in that priority; doesn't fire as a blueprint, unpaid, at allies or their walls, into the fog or through rock; damage-table rows for buildings. The jam mask (built, cleared on death, unaffected allies, rebuilt on share-vision change); sight within and beyond `JamSight`; Radars blind in jammed cells; no Radar ping for a jammed contact.
- **LeakTests:** a unit in a jammed cell beyond `JamSight` never reaches an enemy client (unit, building, terrain, explosion and alert messages); a turret's attack never reaches a client that may not know the turret.
- **PlayMode:** a hosted match with a turret holding a choke and a Jammer hiding a base from a Radar.
- **Performance:** the perf gate's siege load adds turrets and Jammers per owner.

## 7. Decisions

All approved by the owner (2026-10-16): the numbers as starting values to tune in play, area-wide jamming, the 1×1 turret. Changed by the owner: the turret targets units, buildings and walls (§2.1).

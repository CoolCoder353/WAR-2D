# v0.21 Warpath: an AI that uses its army and its money

Planned 2026-11-22. Status: **in development** on `release/v0.21` (2026-11-22); the open decisions below still stand for the owner's review. Decided with the owner: walls on both sides (breaking
in and building), an army that never idles (escalating pressure, map control and split harassment, all three), AI
changes only (the rich-gem economy is flagged, not retuned here), and its own update after v0.20 Onslaught ships, so
the combat rules it reasons about are settled. Everything else below is a proposal; §10 lists the open decisions
with the default this spec assumes.

## 1. Why

The AI plays by a player's rules and fights well once it commits, but in long matches it stalls:

| Symptom | Evidence | Cause in the code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Armies march at targets they can't reach, give up, and pick them again | Playtest 2026-11-10 Server 2 (427 units "0 there" for 4 min; matches hit the 40-minute cap); v0.20 `ai-bench`: 19 of 90 matches capped | `ArmyManager.ChooseTarget` never asks whether a target is reachable; `Blackboard.Unreached` only learns after `MarchSeconds` (240 s) of marching |
| Huge armies sit at the gather point | Playtest Balance 2: "banked, 257k free, Defend, attacks 0" | The attack needs `AttackAdvantage × AttackRatioScale × Caution` over what it remembers; nothing lowers that bar while it waits, and `Maxed` only fires when upkeep room is gone |
| Defences stand where the enemy doesn't come | Owner's report | `DefenceSpots` are chokepoints on lanes towards *known* enemy bases, fixed once; `HomeTurrets` ring the HQ. Where attacks actually came from is never recorded |
| Money piles up | Playtest Balance 2 (9.3 M mean peak balance, Hard), Server 4 and 6 (a lone unplaceable Miner request blocks everything; 9 to 10 idle Builders) | Upkeep caps producers (`BuildingUpkeepShare` 0.6) and units; the only no-upkeep sinks it uses are Miners and a few expansions. There is one HQ per player, and nothing else grows a second base |
| Walls are a Turtle-only afterthought | `BuilderManager.Wall`: one gapped line per chokepoint, Turtle with `DefendsChokepoints` only, never repaired; Hard Diggers dig only walls next to the army | No reach model, no breach plan, no wall repair |

## 2. Scope

**In:** the AI (`Assets/Scripts/Ai/`, `AiView/` read-only helpers, `<Ai>` in `GameConfig.xml`), its dev readouts
(`aistate`, `ai-bench` brief), tests and docs.

**Out:** game rules and balance (no unit, building, cost or income changes; the rich-gem runaway stays an open item,
§9), HUD, networking. The AI keeps its fairness rules: it knows only its `TeamView`, acts only through
`PlayerActions` within its action budget, and every difficulty plays the same policy through handicaps (Hard plays
it as is).

## 3. Shared groundwork: the reach map

Everything below needs one question answered cheaply: *can my units get from A to B over the ground I know, and if
not, which walls are in the way?*

- **`ReachMap`** (new, `Ai/ReachMap.cs`), one per brain, on tiles (2026-11-22: not coarse cells, which would miss a
  one-tile wall): the floor tiles of the map as its side knows it (`view.Tiles`, wall blueprints count as floor),
  labelled into 4-connected components (units can't cut a corner diagonally, so these are exactly where they can
  walk), and the player wall tiles it knows, own and allied included, so its own gaps matter.
- Relabelled when a tile it sees changes between floor and not (dug rock, a wall built or destroyed), spread over
  thinks (`<Ai><Reach><TilesPerThink>`) so 8 Hard AIs on a 1024 map stay inside the tick budget (§8 has the perf
  check); queries use the last finished labels meanwhile.
- **Queries** (cached per component pair until the next relabel):
  - `Query(from, to)`: `Open` (the same component), `Walled` (joined only through walls, with the cheapest **breach**:
    Dijkstra over components and wall tiles, a wall tile costing its last-seen health), or `Cut` (not even through
    walls: rock).
  - `Approaches(to)`: the lanes and chokepoints (`view.Lanes`) a path into a region uses, for §5.

The march-and-give-up rule (`Unreached`) stays as the fallback for what the coarse grid gets wrong.

## 4. Walls

### 4.1 Breaking in (all difficulties, scaled)

- **Target choice** (`ArmyManager.ChooseTarget`, `ScoreTarget`): a `Cut` target is skipped (no more 4-minute marches
  at rock); an `Open` target scores as now; a `Walled` target scores with a breach cost: its worth over
  (1 + breach tiles × wall health / `BreachCostScale`), and only when the group can breach (below).
- **Breach plan** (new `AiPlanKind.Breach` on the `Blackboard`): the group stages at the breach point instead of the
  target, then:
  1. Artillery in the group shells the wall tiles from range (v0.20: it fires over walls), Bombers detonate on the
     breach, Diggers dig it (G on each tile), everyone else attack-moves onto the wall tiles (units attack walls by
     attack-move since v0.16). The existing siege logic (`UpdateSiege`) covers turrets that guard the breach.
  2. Once the reach map shows the breach `Open`, the plan ends and the normal attack runs.
  3. A breach that takes over `BreachSeconds` (90) or costs a bad trade marks the target avoided (`Blackboard.Avoid`).
- **Production:** a known walled enemy raises the mix bias of Diggers and Artillery (`StrategyManager`, as against a
  Turtle today), and the group waits at its staging point until it holds `BreachPower` worth of wall damage per
  second, so it doesn't breach with Tanks alone.
- **Difficulty:** Easy skips `Walled` targets (it gets the `Cut` check only, which already ends the stalls); Normal
  breaches with what its group has; Hard also builds for the breach and times it with a raid elsewhere (§6.3).
  `DigsPastWalls` is folded into this (removed as a separate flag).

### 4.2 Building its own

- **Every style walls**, by a new style number `WallLanes`: Turtle every chokepoint on its lanes out (as now), Balanced
  and Expander the chokepoints on its top threat axes (§5) and its expansions' lane mouths, Rusher none. Easy only
  walls with the Turtle style.
- **A gap for its own army** stays (`WallGap`), sized to its largest unit (2 for 2×2 Snipers and Heavy clearance),
  with a Gun turret placed so it covers the gap and Mines in front of it (the defence spot's turret goes *behind*
  the wall line, not on the lane).
- **Repair and rebuild:** its planned wall tiles (`wallTiles`) are remembered; a destroyed one is re-placed once no
  enemy is within `ArmyRadius` of it, damaged ones are repaired by its Builders out of danger (as buildings are now).
- **Thicker walls as a money sink:** while `Banked`, it adds a second and third layer behind each wall line (5 per
  segment, no upkeep), up to `MaxWallLayers` (style number: Turtle 3, Balanced and Expander 2).

## 5. Defences where the enemy will come from

- **Threat axes** (new on the `Blackboard`): for each of its bases (HQ and forward bases, §7) a score per entry lane:
  - *predicted:* the lanes on the shortest path (`ReachMap.Approaches`) from every enemy base it knows to that base,
    weighted by that enemy's remembered strength; before it knows an enemy, the lanes towards the nearest unseen
    start sites;
  - *observed:* every enemy army it saw approach (a `Threat` near its buildings, an Under-attack alert, a
    `NewArmies` sighting heading in) adds its worth to the lane it came through, decaying with
    `AxisHalfLifeSeconds` (180).
  The axes' weight between predicted and observed scales with Insight (Easy mostly predicted, Hard mostly what it saw).
- **Defence placement** (`BuilderManager`): `DefenceSpots` become the top `DefenceAxes` (style number) lanes by score,
  recomputed every `AxisSeconds` (30). Its defence budget (turrets, Mines, Jammers, walls) is spread over them in
  proportion to the score instead of the same set per spot. A spot that drops out keeps what was built (nothing is
  demolished); new pieces go to the new top spots. `HomeTurrets` still ring the HQ, but the first ones face the top
  axis instead of "towards the enemy".
- **Early warning:** one Radar per base on its top axis (Normal and Hard; `Radar` styles get one per top-two axes).
- **Gather point** (`ArmyManager`): behind the top axis's defence spot instead of in front of the HQ, so the army
  stands where the defences are and the attack comes from.

## 6. An army that never idles

The army's units are split each think by a new **allocator** (`ArmyAllocator`, taking over the split now spread
across `ArmyManager`, `RaidManager` and the home guard) into four jobs, by style shares, so no two managers order the
same unit:

| Job | Share (Balanced default) | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home guard | `HomeGuardShare` (0.2, only after a base threat, as now) | Stands at the gather point; defends |
| Main army | the rest | Gathers, then attacks (§6.1) |
| Posts | `PostShare` (0.2) | Holds ground (§6.2) |
| Raids | `RaidShare` (0.1) | Harasses (§6.3) |

A threat at a base pulls Posts and the Main army back by the existing rule (a real counter-attack recalls; a small
raid doesn't).

### 6.1 Escalating pressure

- **Idle clock:** seconds its Main army has spent in `Gather` while at or over `AttackArmySize` (or maxed), reset by an
  attack.
- **The bar falls:** the advantage it needs is `Need × max(IdleFloor, 1 − idle / IdlePatienceSeconds)` (style numbers:
  Rusher patience 60 s, floor 0.6; Balanced 120 s, 0.75; Expander 180 s, 0.8; Turtle 300 s, 0.9). Caution from bad
  trades still multiplies `Need`, so it doesn't throw armies away twice at the same target (playtest Low 13), and
  avoided targets stay avoided.
- **Banked and big:** with `Banked` and the army over twice `AttackArmySize`, the idle clock runs double.
- **No target it can take:** it sweeps for one (the existing `Sweep`), then sends Posts forward (§6.2) instead of
  waiting.

### 6.2 Map control (posts)

- **Post sites** (scored every `PostSeconds`, 30): central areas with rich gems (`AiRegion.RichTiles`), free
  expansions near enemies, lane junctions between it and its nearest enemy, and enemy expansions it has seen. Score:
  worth (gems, enemy Miners denied) over distance from its nearest base, minus known enemy defence there.
- **Holding:** a post group attack-moves to the site and stays; enemy Miners, blueprints and Builders it sees within
  `PostRadius` are attacked (Builders and blueprints first: they deny expansions). A post that meets a force it can't
  beat (fight model) falls back to the nearest base and the site is avoided for a while.
- **Count:** `MaxPosts` by difficulty (Easy 0, Normal 1, Hard 3), and only once its Main army has `AttackArmySize`.
- Posts double as forward scouting: what they see feeds the enemy models and targets.

### 6.3 Split harassment

- `RaidManager` grows from one raid to up to `MaxRaids` at once (Easy 0, Normal 1, Hard 3), each a small group of its
  fastest units (Scouts, Bombers, Flamethrowers), on different targets: enemy Miners and expansions far from where its
  Main army is going.
- **Timed:** Hard launches raids when its Main army engages or starts a breach, so defenders are split.
- Raids keep today's rules otherwise (retreat when outmatched, cooldowns, Turtle factor).

## 7. Spending the money: branching out

### 7.1 The spending ladder

When `Banked` (over `BankResources` for `BankSeconds`), the AI works down an ordered list and takes the first rung it
can, every think, until it isn't banked:

1. Fill production queues (exists).
2. More producers, within `BuildingUpkeepShare` (exists).
3. **Another expansion** (no claim cap while banked; §7.3 picks it).
4. **Grow an expansion into a forward base** (§7.2).
5. **Fortify:** defences at its axes and posts beyond the style's count, thicker walls (§4.2). Walls cost no upkeep
   and Mines little, so this rung still works when upkeep is maxed.
6. Gift to a teammate (exists).

Fixes in the same area (playtest Server 4 and 6): a building request that can't be placed (`IsSaturated`) no longer
blocks the rest, it falls through to the next rung; idle Builders are given to the expansions and forward bases that
want them instead of waiting on an unplaceable Miner.

### 7.2 Forward bases

There is one HQ per player, so a "new base" is an expansion grown into a base:

- **What it has:** Miners on its gems, `ForwardProducers` producers (style: Rusher 2, Balanced 1, Expander 1, Turtle
  0 unless banked), a Radar, a Gun turret per entry lane plus walls on its lane mouths (by §4.2 and §5, as for the
  HQ), its own Builders (`BuildersPerExpansion` stay there), and its own rally point.
- **Which one:** among the expansions it holds, the one closest to its top threat axis's enemy (shortest
  reinforcement to the front) that its army can hold; Rusher picks the closest to the enemy even if unheld, Turtle
  the safest.
- **Reinforcement:** new units from a forward base's producers join the Main army or the posts near it, so the front
  is fed without the cross-map walk.
- **Losing it:** a forward base under a threat it can't hold calls the Main army (as an expansion does now); a lost
  one isn't rebuilt for `RebuildAfterSeconds` (240).

### 7.3 Expansion choice

The existing score (gem worth over distance, `ExpansionDistanceScale`) gains **safety**: minus the observed threat on
the lanes into it (§5) and the enemy strength near it. Expander favours worth, Turtle safety, Rusher closeness to the
enemy.

## 8. Difficulty and style numbers, config, readouts, tests

- **Config:** every new number in `GameConfig.xml` `<Ai>` (`<Reach>`, and new `<Play>`, `<Policy>`, style and
  difficulty fields), parsed strictly through `AiConfig`; no hard-coded numbers.
- **`aistate`** gains: `idle` (seconds) and `need` (the advantage it wants now), `posts`, `raids` (list),
  `axes` (top lanes and scores), `breach` (target and progress), `bases` (forward bases), `ladder` (the rung it is on),
  `reach` (cells rebuilt, queries this think).
- **`ai-bench --brief`** gains per difficulty: mean idle army seconds per match, peak and mean banked, share of income
  spent, give-ups, breaches started and won, forward bases built, capped matches.
- **Tests:**
  - EditMode: `ReachMap` (open, walled with the cheapest breach, cut; incremental rebuild matches a full one),
    threat-axis scoring and decay, the idle bar's decay and floor, the allocator's shares (no unit in two jobs), the
    spending ladder's order and fall-through on a saturated request.
  - PlayMode (game-time waits through `PlayModeMatch.WaitUntil`): a Hard AI breaches a walled-in HQ within a set time;
    a banked AI builds a forward base with a producer; an AI hit twice from one lane puts its next turret there; a
    big idle army attacks within its patience.
  - `LeakTests` unchanged (the AI reads only its `TeamView`); a test that the reach map uses only walls its side saw.
- **Performance:** `tools/perf-run.sh` with 8 Hard AIs on 1024 before and after the reach map (`perf-compare.py`, three
  runs a side); the reach map's rebuild is sliced so no think exceeds `Brain` think budget.

## 9. Exit criteria

- `ai-bench`: the difficulty ladder holds (Hard beats Normal, Normal beats Easy, at least v0.20's rates); capped
  matches down from 19 of 90 to at most 5; no "gave up" on the same tile twice in a match; mean idle army seconds and
  peak banked balance each at most half of the v0.20 baseline (taken in phase 0).
- Both suites pass (`tools/check.sh`), perf gate no worse than v0.20's run on the same machine.
- A Linux match against Hard on 512 and 1024 where it walls, breaches, holds a post, raids and builds a forward base,
  watched through `tools/war2d`.
- Docs: `gameplay.md` "AI players", `tuning.md` (the new numbers), `known-issues.md` and `bug-review.md` for what
  this fixes.
- **Flagged, not done here:** the rich-gem economy (375/s per rich Miner, millions banked by minute 30). Teaching the
  AI to spend will lower its banked balance but can't make money mean something again; that is a balance task for
  `tools/balance-sheet.js` and `ai-tune`, to schedule separately.

## 10. Phases

Each phase lands on its own branch with both suites passing and an `ai-bench --brief` compared to the last.

| Phase | Work | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Readouts (`aistate` fields, bench metrics) and a v0.20 baseline bench | Measure before changing |
| 1 | `ReachMap`; `Cut` targets skipped; breach plan (§3, §4.1) | Ends the stalls and capped matches, the worst symptom |
| 2 | `ArmyAllocator` and escalating pressure (§6, §6.1) | The other half of "sits on its army"; the allocator is needed by 4 and 5 |
| 3 | Threat axes, defence placement, gather point (§5) | Uses the reach map's approaches |
| 4 | Posts and split raids (§6.2, §6.3) | Needs the allocator |
| 5 | Spending ladder, request fall-through, forward bases, expansion safety (§7) | Uses axes (where to fortify) and posts (holding them) |
| 6 | Own walls: every style, gaps covered, repair, layers (§4.2) | Uses axes; also a money sink for 5 |
| 7 | Tune the new numbers with `ai-tune`, exit checks, docs (§9) | Last, with everything in |

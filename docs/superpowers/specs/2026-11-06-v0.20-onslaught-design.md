# v0.20 Onslaught: Design

**Status:** written 2026-11-06 from the owner's answers in that day's planning session, then revised the same day with the owner's review (a wider, longer flame; Bombers 99 % blast resistant; Artillery firing over walls; a 2×2 Gun turret; longer Sniper and Artillery range; the asset review, §8.4; turning heads kept, §8.3). Nothing is built. Every number in §11 is a **starting value** to tune with `balance-sheet` and `ai-bench` before landing. Decisions the owner didn't make are in §15 with their defaults. The implementation plan goes in `superpowers/plans/` when the update starts, after v0.19 has shipped.
**Parent:** [roadmap](../../roadmap.md) §2 v0.20. **Figma brief:** [ux/v0.20-figma.md](../../ux/v0.20-figma.md).

## 1. Goal

A combat overhaul in two halves:

1. **Each unit fights its own way.** Four units are reworked from the owner's brief: the **Bomber** (§2), **Flamethrower** (§3), **Artillery** (§4) and **Sniper** (§5). Four more are given a clearer job: the **Scout**, **Heavy**, **Gun turret** and **Tank** (§6). Three shared rules carry them: **blast resistance**, **flat armour** and **burning** (§7). The Bomb building is renamed the **Mine** (§2.6).
2. **Every fight shows what happened.** New **deaths and wrecks**, new **attack effects** for each rework, and a **combat sound pass** (§8, §9). v0.19's turning turret heads stay on every unit that has one, and the new 2×2 Sniper and Gun turret get one too (§8.3).
3. **Nothing new lands unseen.** Every new or changed sprite, effect, icon and sound is reviewed and approved by the owner before it goes in (§8.4).

The owner listed the four reworks as "just some of the changes" and chose the other four units' roles, the visual scope and the HUD round on 2026-11-06 (§14).

**Out of scope** (owner, 2026-11-06): damage states on hurt units and buildings (smoke and flames by health), fire spreading from unit to unit, PR 42's shells and PR 38's bomber chain rule as built (v0.20 designs both fresh), PR 43's Large Radar and Jammer, new unit types, a fire-at-ground order.

**The leak rule:** every new state a client draws (burning, leaping, aiming, a shell in flight, a death's cause) travels only with what the client is already sent: units and buildings on fog cells its side sees now, and attack events under their existing rule. A client that doesn't see an Artillery or a Sniper is never told where it is (§4.3, §5.3). `LeakTests` gain the cases in §13.

## 2. Bomber (was Suicide bomber)

### 2.1 Running in

On an Attack-move, or idle with an enemy in sight, a Bomber searches its sight as now and runs at the **centre of the nearest army**. That army is every enemy unit its side sees within `Bomber/ArmyTiles` of the nearest such unit, and its centre is their average position. With no enemy unit in sight it runs at the nearest enemy building, aiming at the middle of a side (v0.18), and at an enemy wall it explodes on contact as now.

v0.18's "point of most worth" aim (`Bomber/AimTiles`, `AimReachTiles`, `GoodEnough`, `FriendlyWeight`) is retired.

### 2.2 The leap

- **When:** the nearest enemy unit is within the Bomber's `Range` (now the leap trigger), or, against a building, the middle of its side is.
- **Where:** the army's centre, or the point `Bomber/LeapReach` along the line to it when the centre is further. Against a building, the middle of its side.
- **Path:** a leap passes over units, own and enemy, but **not over walls, rock or buildings** (owner). The straight line to the landing point must cross none of them. If it does, the Bomber keeps running and tries again on its next search.
- **In the air:** the leap takes `Bomber/LeapSeconds` whatever its length. While airborne the Bomber can't be targeted, takes no damage of any kind (gunfire, blasts, fire, decay), isn't pushed and pushes nothing. Orders are ignored until it lands, Detonate included.
- **Landing:** it explodes the moment it lands, at the landing point it chose. It doesn't follow the army once it has jumped.

### 2.3 Going off before the leap

A Bomber on the ground **explodes whenever it dies**: gunfire, a blast, fire or decay (owner). It also explodes when brought to `Bomber/LastGaspHealth` (10 %) or less, as in v0.18. The Detonate order (`B`) still sets off every selected Bomber that isn't airborne. Whoever killed it gets the kill, and what its blast kills is credited to its owner (unchanged).

### 2.4 Blast resistance and chains

Bombers shrug off **99 %** of every blast (owner): `BlastResist` 0.99 (§7.1) against other Bombers, Artillery shells and Mines alike. A Bomber blast (150) does 1.5 to a Bomber, and a shell's centre (160) 1.6. So blasts practically never set off a Bomber: a full-health one (30) would need about 18 to reach its last gasp (3), and only a Bomber already shot down to a few points goes up from a neighbour's blast. Packs don't chain, and Artillery is no longer an answer to Bombers: gunfire and Gun turrets are (§6.3). `Bomber/ChainPasses` (4 rounds a tick) stays for the rare chain.

### 2.5 The blast

Unchanged: `BlastRadius` 2, `BlastDamage` 150, the damage table's multipliers, own side and allies at `Bomber/FriendlyDamage`, blast resistance applied (§7.1).

### 2.6 Renames

| Was | Now |
| --- | --- |
| `UnitType.SuicideBomber`, "Suicide bomber" | `UnitType.Bomber`, **"Bomber"** (same enum value) |
| `BuildingType.Bomb`, "Bomb" | `BuildingType.Mine`, **"Mine"** (same enum value) |
| `Siege/BombTriggerRadius`, `Siege/BombFriendlyDamage` | `Siege/MineTriggerRadius`, `Siege/MineFriendlyDamage` |
| Alert "Bomb triggered" (`AlertKind.BombTriggered`) | "Mine triggered" (`AlertKind.MineTriggered`) |
| `bomb_blast`, `alert_bomb` sounds; Bomb art | `mine_blast`, `alert_mine`; Mine art (the same look, renamed generator) |

Config `type` attributes, `ArtManifest` keys, `HoverText`, the Field manual's words, the command card's button and its icon, the AI's `ArmyMix`, `Counter` and raid lists, `gameplay.md` and `tuning.md` all move to the new names. The parser is strict, so a config with an old name fails with an error naming the new one (`ConfigParserTests`). The enum values don't change, so settings and bindings carry over.

## 3. Flamethrower

- **Longer range, a wider spread, much less damage per hit:** `Range` 2.5 → **5.5**, `Splash` 1.5 → **2.5** (owner review: a bigger spread and a little more range), `Damage` 6 → 2 a hit (every 0.5 s). The flame stream and its fire are drawn over the real splash circle, as in v0.19, so the wider spread shows.
- **Every hit sets the target on fire**, the direct hit and every splash hit alike (§7.3): `Burn/Dps` 6 for `Burn/Seconds` 4. Fire doesn't stack: a burning target hit again burns at the same rate, with its timer reset (owner).
- **What burns** (owner): light units at the full rate, heavy units at half (`Burn/HeavyUnit` 0.5), buildings at `Burn/Building` (1.0). Walls don't burn. Fire doesn't spread from one unit to the next.
- **Damage per second against a light unit**: 2 × 1.5 (damage table) / 0.5 s = 6 from the flame, plus 6 from the fire, 12 in all, against 18 today. The fire keeps hurting for 4 s after the stream stops, and the 2.5-tile spread lights far more of a group at once (about 2.8 times the area of today's 1.5), so a Flamethrower is worse at killing one unit fast and much better at leaving a whole group worn down and in reach of everyone else.

## 4. Artillery

### 4.1 Reach and sight

`Range` 18 → **36**, `Sight` stays **18**, `MinRange` 4 → 6. Units only fire at what their side sees now (unchanged), so beyond 18 tiles an Artillery needs **spotters**: Scouts (§6.1), Radars, allies who share vision, or any of its own units further forward. The v0.18 playtest rule that "Artillery and Snipers see as far as they shoot" ends here.

**Over walls** (owner review): an Artillery's line of fire ignores walls, so it can shell a target behind a wall. Its **sight does not** go over walls (walls block sight for every unit, as now), so the target must be seen by something else, such as a Scout looking round the wall, a Radar, or an ally. Rock still blocks the shot (§15 D17).

### 4.2 Shells fly and land

- **Firing:** at a target its side sees, in range, past its minimum range, with no rock on the line (walls don't count). It aims where the target will be when the shell lands, from the target's driving velocity (`Artillery/LeadAim`), so a unit driving straight is hit and one that turns or stops can get away. The landing point may be past `Range`, capped at `Range + Artillery/LeadCapTiles`.
- **Flight:** at `Artillery/ShellSpeed` (14 tiles a second, so about 2.6 s at full range) along the arc v0.19 already draws. A shell in flight lands even if its Artillery dies, and its kills go to the Artillery's owner.
- **Landing:** a blast of radius `BlastRadius` (2.0 → **3.5**) and `BlastDamage` (70 → **160**), 100 % at the centre falling to 50 % at the edge, times the damage table (buildings ×2, walls ×1). Enemies take all of it, less their blast resistance (§7.1). **Own and allied units and buildings take `Artillery/FriendlyDamage` (25 %)** of it (owner). There is no direct hit any more: the blast is the whole shot.
- **Reload:** `AttackInterval` 5 → **15 s**.

### 4.3 What clients are told

A shell goes to each client under the attack-event rule. A client that sees the Artillery when it fires is sent the whole flight. A client that sees only the landing point is sent just the shell's last `Artillery/IncomingSeconds` (0.6 s), drawn falling steeply onto the point, so it learns where the shell lands but not where it came from. A client that sees neither is sent nothing. The crater and blast follow what the client sees, as deaths do.

## 5. Sniper

### 5.1 Body

A **2×2 unit**: `Radius` 0.35 → 0.7 and `SizeClass` 0 → 1 (the large pathing class, as for Heavies and Artillery; it leaves the Heavy Factory on a tile with 2 tiles of clearance). `MoveSpeed` 4 → 3, `Health` 70 → 140, armour class stays Light.

### 5.2 Stop and aim

- **Long range, middling sight:** `Range` 20 → **28**, `Sight` 20 → **12**, so it too wants spotters. `MinRange` 3 → **5**: it still can't fire up close and needs a screen (owner).
- **Aim:** once its reload is over, a Sniper with a target its side sees, in range, aims for `Sniper/AimSeconds` (2 s). It must stand still the whole time. Moving, losing the target from sight or range, or changing target starts the aim again. When the aim completes it fires. On a Move order it doesn't aim. On an Attack-move it stops to aim as other units stop to fight. Hold aims.
- **The shot:** `Damage` 60 → **160**, `AttackInterval` 3 → **5 s** after each shot, so about 7 s from shot to shot. Damage table: light units 1 → **0.5**, heavy units 0.7 → **2.0**, buildings 0.25 and walls 0.05 unchanged. One shot nearly kills a Tank (80 of 100); two kill a Heavy (2 × (320 − 4 armour) of 400).

### 5.3 The laser

While aiming, a thin laser runs from the Sniper's barrel to its target. It brightens as the aim completes, and a glint shows at the muzzle. It is an attack event: a client that sees the Sniper is sent it. A client that sees only the target is sent nothing, so a hidden Sniper stays hidden until it fires, and then the shot's trail shows only where the client sees.

## 6. Scout, Heavy, Gun turret, Tank

### 6.1 Scout: the spotter

`Sight` 12 → **16**, the longest of any unit, so a Scout ahead of a gun line lets Artillery (§4.1) and Snipers (§5.2) fire to their full range. Everything else stays. The manual's Scout page and the AI (§10) treat spotting as its job.

### 6.2 Heavy: armoured

- **Flat armour** `FlatArmour` **4** (§7.2): each hit that isn't a blast or fire loses 4, down to 1. A Scout's shot (4 × 0.25) and a Flamethrower's (2 × 0.5) do 1; a Tank's (10) does 6; a Sniper's does 316.
- **Blast resistance** `BlastResist` **0.5**.
- Fire burns it at half rate (§3), which ignores the flat armour (owner: the half rate is the Heavy's protection from fire).

### 6.3 Gun turret: against swarms

A bigger, costlier, slower-to-build turret that fires very fast (owner review):

- **Footprint** 1×1 → **2×2**, `Health` 500 → **1,000** (double, for a footprint four times the size and a much higher cost).
- **Fire:** `AttackInterval` 0.4 → **0.1 s** (ten shots a second), `Damage` 12 → **6** a shot, so 60 damage a second before the damage table (today: 30). Damage table unchanged: light units 1.5 (90 a second), heavy units 0.5 (3 a shot, 1 after a Heavy's armour, so Heavies and Artillery stay its counters), buildings 0.5, walls 0.25. `Range` 9 and `Sight` 10 unchanged.
- **Cost and build:** `UpfrontCost` 150 → **400**, `RunningCost` 4 → **8**, and a new per-building `BuildSeconds` of **90 s** with one Builder (several Builders share it, as now). Today a building's build time is its health over `Siege/BuildPower`, which would give the turret 40 s; `BuildSeconds`, where given, replaces that, and every other building keeps the health rule.
- **Targets:** a **Bomber in range first**, then the nearest unit, then buildings, then walls. A Bomber in the air can't be targeted (§2.2), so a turret has to kill it on the way in, which is now its main job: blasts no longer hurt Bombers (§2.4).
- **Its head turns** to its target, like the units' turrets (§8.3).

### 6.4 Tank: the baseline

No rule changes. It is the unit the others are measured against, and only its numbers may move in the balance pass. It is the one unit that does full damage to every target class and moves at a middling speed.

## 7. Shared rules

### 7.1 Blast resistance

A per-unit `<BlastResist>` (0 to 0.99, default 0): the share of any **blast** a unit shrugs off. A blast is a Bomber's explosion, an Artillery shell's landing or a Mine. Flame splash is not a blast. Starting values: **Bomber 0.99** (owner), **Heavy 0.5, Artillery 0.25**, every other unit 0 (owner: "Bombers high, Heavies some, light units none"). Buildings and walls have none; the damage table sets what blasts do to them.

### 7.2 Flat armour

A per-unit `<FlatArmour>` (default 0): taken off every hit that is **not a blast or fire**, after the damage table. A hit never does less than `Combat/MinHit` (1). Only the Heavy has it at the start.

### 7.3 Burning

- A unit or building hit by a Flamethrower (direct or splash) **burns** for `Burn/Seconds`, taking `Burn/Dps` times its class rate (`Burn/LightUnit` 1, `Burn/HeavyUnit` 0.5, `Burn/Building` 1.0; walls never) each second, applied once a tick. Fire ignores flat armour and blast resistance.
- **One fire per target:** a new flame hit resets the timer and nothing else.
- **Credit:** fire damage and fire kills go to the owner of the Flamethrower that lit it last. A burning Bomber that dies explodes (§2.3). Fire raises the usual Under-attack alerts, with their throttle.
- **Nothing puts it out** but time. Builders can repair a burning building while it burns.

### 7.4 How one hit is worked out

```
direct hit (gunfire, a flame's direct and splash hits):
    max(Combat/MinHit, Damage × DamageTable[attacker, class] − FlatArmour)
blast (Bomber, Artillery shell, Mine):
    BlastDamage × DamageTable[attacker, class] × falloff × (1 − BlastResist) × (own or allied ? FriendlyDamage : 1)
fire, per second:
    Burn/Dps × Burn/<class>
```

`ApplyDamageJob` stays the only writer of health: shell landings, fire and leap blasts go into the same hit queue.

## 8. Visuals

All client-side, drawn only from what the client is sent (the leak rule), generated (`RenderGen`, `EffectGen`, registered in `Editor/ArtBuild.cs`), crisp at every zoom, and respecting Reduce motion where something flashes. Before each effect lands the owner approves it on the asset review page, as frame strips and clips from a real match (§8.4).

### 8.1 Deaths and wrecks

v0.19 already leaves a scorch and a charred wreck (`Decals`) for every unit, and rubble for a building. v0.20 adds:

- **A death by cause:** the death event gains a cause (shot, blast, fire). **Shot:** a pop and, for units with a turret, the turret thrown off to land beside the wreck. **Blast:** the wreck flung up to a tile away from the blast centre and turned, with debris. **Fire:** the unit burns down, flames on the sprite for a moment before it collapses into a blacker wreck that smokes for `Effects/SmoulderSeconds`.
- **By size:** small units (Scout, Tank, Builder, Digger, Flamethrower) get a small explosion. Large units (Heavy, Artillery, Sniper) get a large one with a second blast a beat later and a larger scorch. Bombers leave no wreck, as now.
- **Buildings collapse in stages** over about 1.5 s: small explosions across the footprint, the sprite darkening and sinking into the rubble decal, a dust cloud. The HQ ends with a larger blast.

### 8.2 Attack effects

| Rule | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Bomber leap (§2.2) | A short crouch, then the sprite rises on an arc with its shadow on the ground (the shell's height cue from v0.19), with a spark trail; the landing blast is larger than today's with a shockwave ring sized to `BlastRadius`. |
| Burning (§7.3) | Flickering flames over a burning unit's or building's sprite, embers and a thin smoke column; scorch marks left on the ground along a flame stream. Capped at `Effects/MaxFires` drawn at once, nearest the view's centre first. |
| Sniper aim and shot (§5.3) | The laser (thin, brightening, a muzzle glint); the shot as a bright hard line that lingers 0.3 s and a hit spark; a Heavy's armour taking a hit shows a spark ring, so armour reads as armour. |
| Artillery (§4) | A launch puff, a larger shell, the near-vertical incoming drop for clients that don't see the gun (§4.3), a blast with a shockwave ring and dust sized to `BlastRadius`, and a larger crater. |
| Flat armour (§7.2) | The spark ring above on any hit armour shrinks to 1, so small arms visibly bounce off Heavies. |
| Gun turret (§6.3) | A new 2×2 building sprite with a separate turning head and a twin muzzle that alternates flashes; at ten shots a second its tracers merge into a short stream rather than drawing every shot. |

### 8.3 Turning heads (owner: kept through v0.20)

v0.19's turning turrets stay on the Scout, Tank, Sniper, Heavy and Artillery: the head swings at its limited rate toward what the unit fires at, and back to the hull's facing after. Nothing in v0.20 may drop it:

- **The 2×2 Sniper** is a new sprite (§5.1) drawn with a separate head, like the Heavy and Artillery. Its head turns to its target and holds there while it aims; the laser (§5.3) and the shot leave from the head's barrel tip.
- **The 2×2 Gun turret** gets a turning head too (it has none today), on the same rule and the same instanced head layer.
- **The Artillery** keeps its raised barrel; a shot over a wall (§4.1) raises it the same way.
- **Deaths** (§8.1) throw off the head the unit had at its last facing.
- `TurretAimTests` cover the Sniper and the Gun turret as well as today's five units, and the perf gate's client frame is measured with every head drawn.

### 8.4 Asset review (owner approval)

Every new or changed asset is approved by the owner **before it lands**, tracked in [art/v0.20-asset-review.md](../../art/v0.20-asset-review.md) (one row per asset, with its state: drafted, awaiting approval, approved, or changes asked):

- **What is reviewed:** unit and building sprites (the 2×2 Sniper, the 2×2 Gun turret, the renamed Bomber and Mine), effects (§8.1, §8.2), HUD icons and markers (the [Figma brief](../../ux/v0.20-figma.md)'s B1 and B2), and sounds (§9).
- **How:** each batch is generated from its `RenderGen`, `EffectGen` or sound JSON and gathered on one **review page** (a private artifact link for the owner, rebuilt for each batch): sprites in every player colour and facing at 1×, 2× and 4× zoom, effects as frame strips and short clips taken from a real match through the control API, sounds as players. The owner approves or comments there or in conversation, and the checklist records the decision and the date.
- **Order:** the new unit and building sprites first (the Sniper and the Gun turret need their heads before the aim and turret work is checked), then the effects of each rework as its rules are built, then the sounds.
- **Nothing ships unapproved:** v0.20 doesn't merge while any row is not approved. HUD frames are approved in Figma as usual, and the manual's words through its mock.

## 10. AI

- **Fight model:** `Ai/FightModel` and `tools/balance-sheet.js` learn the new rules: fire as damage over time, flat armour, blast resistance, the Sniper's aim time added to its interval, the Artillery's reload, blast and the share of shells a moving target dodges (`Ai/ShellHitShare`), and the Bomber's leap (it can't be shot in the air, so only its run in counts against it). Both tools must keep agreeing (the existing check).
- **Spotters:** Normal and Hard keep `Ai/Play/Spotters` Scouts (1 per gun group) a few tiles ahead of their Artillery and Snipers. Their siege of out-ranged turrets (v0.14) uses the spotters' sight. Easy doesn't.
- **Counters:** `ArmyMix` and `Counters` are updated to the new roles: Snipers against Heavies, Flamethrowers and Gun turrets against light swarms and Bombers, Artillery against turrets (now over their walls too), clumps and buildings, Bombers against packed armies.
- **Turrets:** Hard's opening of three Gun turrets in front of its HQ doesn't survive a 400-cost, 90-second turret. The style build orders and `Ai/Play` turret counts are retuned (fewer turrets, started earlier, on the chokepoints it already finds), and the AI places a 2×2 footprint.
- **Bombers and leaps** need no AI code: the leap is a sim rule, so the AI's Bombers leap like a player's.
- **Check:** `ai-bench.sh --brief` shows the difficulty ladder holding (Hard beats Normal beats Easy), with no style winning everything.

# v0.16 Clarity: Design

**Status:** written 2026-11-01 from the owner's choices in that day's planning session; **approved by the owner on 2026-11-01** with the default open decisions. Numbers are starting values to tune in play. Not started; the implementation plan is written when v0.16 starts.
**Parent:** [roadmap](../../roadmap.md) §2 v0.16. **Figma brief:** [ux/v0.16-figma.md](../../ux/v0.16-figma.md).

## 1. Goal

Make the battle easier to read, armies easier to move, and the front end look finished:

1. **Reading the battle** (client only): target lines, strategic zoom, fights on the minimap, and a clear HQ-placement minimap.
2. **Controls** (server): formations, and Builders that repair and build nearby on their own when idle (which also fixes the long wall drag).
3. **Menu presentation:** transitions and motion through the menus, and a better-looking lobby.

**Out of scope** (roadmap §5): status markers, a Patrol order, repeat production, unit animation, dynamic music, ambient sound, a tutorial. Nothing from the owner's 2026-10-30 "Not pursued" list ([polish.md](../../polish.md)).

**One rule for everything in §2:** it is drawn only from what the client already receives (its own units and orders, replicated units, buildings, fog, attack events). Nothing new is sent to clients for it, so `LeakTests` stay as they are.

## 2. Reading the battle

### 2.1 Target lines

- **What:** for the player's **selected** own units and Gun turrets, a thin line from each to what it is shooting at, while it keeps shooting. A unit on attack-move with no target yet gets a fainter dashed line to its attack-move goal.
- **Data:** the attack events the client already gets (`ClientUnitStore`'s `AttackEvent`: attacker id, and the target id only when the client may know the target, `ReplicationEncoder.MayKnowTarget`). A line is drawn from an event of a selected attacker no older than `TargetLines/HoldSeconds` (1.5 s), to the target's drawn position (`ClientWorld` for a unit, `ClientBuildings` for a building, the tile for a wall). An event with no named target draws no line, the same rule as tracers. The attack-move goal is the one this client sent (`Selection` remembers it per unit until a new order), so nothing new is needed from the server.
- **Look:** 1 px at every zoom, red towards units and buildings, orange towards walls, 60 % alpha; at most `TargetLines/Max` (200) lines, the selection's nearest to the view's centre first. Drawn with the existing `OverlayMesh` (`Client/Overlays/`), under the HUD and over units.
- **Setting:** Gameplay → Target lines: Selected (default), Off.

### 2.2 Strategic zoom

- **What:** zoomed out past the point where a unit is a few pixels across, units draw as **type icons** and buildings as **symbols**, in team colours, at a fixed screen size, so a 1024 map reads like a board. Zooming back in returns to the art at once.
- **Threshold:** when `CameraZoom.Scale` (screen pixels per art pixel) falls below `StrategicZoom/Below` (0.35). One threshold, no blend (pixels stay crisp); a 10 % hysteresis band stops flicker at the boundary.
- **Drawing:** the same instanced renderers (`InstancedUnitRenderer`, `InstancedBuildingRenderer`) with a second frame set: icon frames in a new `Strategic` section of the Effects atlas, generated from 8×8 bitmaps in `Art/PixelIcons.cs` (drawn in Figma first). Each instance's `scale` is set so the icon is `StrategicZoom/IconPixels` (10) screen pixels for units and the footprint's on-screen size (at least 12 px) for buildings. The team colour comes through the existing mask path. Ghosts stay dimmed. Selection rings, the selected unit's target lines and order overlays still draw; health bars, decals and small effects (sparks, puffs, tracers) are hidden while strategic.
- **Setting:** Gameplay → Strategic zoom: On (default), Off.

### 2.3 Fights on the minimap

- **What:** a short pulse on the minimap where a fight happens that the player's side can see, so a skirmish off screen gets noticed.
- **Data:** the same attack events (they reach the client only for attackers its side may know). Each event's attacker position is binned into a `MinimapCombat/CellTiles` (16) cell; a cell with any event pulses for `MinimapCombat/PulseSeconds` (1.5) and then can't pulse again for `MinimapCombat/QuietSeconds` (3). The pulse is drawn into `MinimapTexture` at its 5 Hz redraw (a ring growing over the pulse), in the alert colour when the player's own units or buildings are involved and white otherwise. At most `MinimapCombat/Max` (32) at once.
- No sound and no alert row: the existing Under-attack alert already covers your own losses.

### 2.4 The HQ-placement minimap (owner, 2026-11-01)

The owner wants the minimap, while you place your HQ, to show the whole map and the options you have. The 2026-10-26 fix (`MinimapController.Redraw` with `revealTerrain`, `PlotMarker`) does draw the terrain and the options, but its markers are 2 texels across, so on a 512 or 1024 map they are specks.

- **First:** look at it in a running match through the control API (`tools/war2d`, screenshot during _PlacingHQ_) on every map size, and record what is wrong.
- **Then, whatever the check finds:** the whole map's terrain is drawn as explored (not dimmed to near-black) while placing; each of the player's own options is a marker at least `PlacementMinimap/MarkerPixels` (8) screen pixels across, numbered to match its Option button; teammates' options (which the player is told) show in their colours, smaller; a click on an option's marker moves the camera there (it already moves the camera; the click now snaps to the nearest option within its marker). The pick timer stays on the HUD overlay.
- Nothing else is shown: other players' options are never sent (v0.11's leak tests stand).

## 3. Controls

### 3.1 Formations

- **What:** a group given a Move or Attack-move arrives **together and in a block**: it travels at its slowest member's speed, and at the goal each unit takes its own place, heavier units at the front (towards the order's direction) and ranged units behind.
- **When:** for any order of two or more units through `CmdOrderChunk` or `CmdOrderSquad` while the sender's Gameplay → Formations setting is on (default on). The order commands gain a `formation` flag (a bool; the server validates nothing more about it). A single unit, Build, Repair, Dig, Hold and Stop never form up.
- **Server, at the boundary** (`SimCommandSystem`, when it applies the order): it sorts the group by role (front: Heavies, Tanks, Flamethrowers and bombers; middle: Scouts, Builders, Diggers; back: Snipers and Artillery), lays a block of slots behind the goal facing from the group's centre towards the goal (`Formation/Spacing` 1.2 tiles, rows of about √n, wider than deep), moves each slot onto open floor (`OrderBook`'s nearest-open-ground rule, within the slot spacing) and stores per unit a **slot offset** and the order's **group speed** (the slowest member's `MoveSpeed`). Two new `SimData` per-unit arrays (`FormationOffset`, `FormationSpeed`), written only at the boundary.
- **Movement stage:** a unit with a group speed caps its speed to it while it is farther than `Formation/LooseTiles` (4) from its slot (so a group doesn't crawl through its last few tiles); within `Formation/SlotTiles` (12) of the goal it steers to goal + offset instead of the goal (the flow field still carries it there). Arrival is reaching its slot. A unit that falls `Formation/LostTiles` (20) behind the group's leader drops its cap and catches up at full speed.
- **Ends** when the unit arrives, gets a new order, or its target search takes over (an Attack-move unit that finds an enemy fights as today; once the fight is over it walks to its slot, not the bare goal).
- **The AI** orders without formations (it stages its own attacks); a later tune can turn them on for it.

### 3.2 Builders build and repair on their own

- **What:** a Builder with no order (idle: not moving, nothing queued) works on its own on the nearest of its owner's **blueprints** and **wall blueprints** within `AutoWork/Radius` (8) tiles, then on the nearest damaged own building or wall segment below `AutoWork/RepairBelow` (0.9 of its health), at the normal cost through the existing work and repair queues (repairs stop when the money does, with the Repairs stopped alert, as now).
- **Where:** `SimConstructionSystem`'s Builder job, for idle Builders only, sliced over ticks like the target search (`AutoWork/SliceTicks` 10: each idle Builder looks once every half second). It reads the building spatial hash and the wall state arrays it already reads.
- **It never** takes a Builder off an order, walks it further than the radius, or works on a teammate's buildings (they are their owner's to order).
- **Setting:** none per player; on for everyone (the AI's Builder manager keeps ordering its Builders, so they are never idle for long).
- **The long wall drag:** today a wall drag sends Build orders only for its first `Orders/MaxQueued + 1` tiles ([bug-review.md](../../bug-review.md), v0.7 branch review). The drag still lays every blueprint; Builders that finish their queued tiles are idle beside the rest of the line, so they carry on along it on their own. A drag longer than the radius from where the Builders stand gets them moving along it, segment by segment, because each segment they finish leaves the next one within reach.

## 4. Menu presentation

Figma first; nothing is built before the owner approves the frames ([brief](../../ux/v0.16-figma.md) §M).

- **Transitions:** moving between the main menu, Play, Lobby, Settings and the Replays screen slides and fades (150–250 ms, eased); dialogs scale in from 96 %; buttons get a pressed state and a hover lift. UI Toolkit USS transitions only (`transition-property`), no new runtime animation code beyond class toggles.
- **The lobby:** player rows become cards (colour swatch, name, team, ready, the AI tag and its difficulty and style), the map preview grows and carries the map size and seed, and the match settings group into Map, Start and Rules blocks. Same controls and behaviour as today.
- **Main menu:** the title, the live menu battle behind it (unchanged), and the buttons' entrance motion.
- **Reduce motion:** Settings → Graphics → Reduce motion turns the transitions into instant swaps (default off).

## 5. HUD and settings (Figma first)

Target-line colours, the strategic icon set (one per unit type, one symbol per building type), the minimap pulse, the HQ-placement minimap markers, the Gameplay settings rows (Target lines, Strategic zoom, Formations) and the Graphics row (Reduce motion), and every §4 frame. The icons are drawn in Figma and copied into `Art/PixelIcons.cs` (`NoPlaceholderArtTests` still holds: nothing hand-made under `Resources`).

## 6. Config

```xml
<TargetLines><HoldSeconds>1.5</HoldSeconds><Max>200</Max></TargetLines>
<StrategicZoom><Below>0.35</Below><IconPixels>10</IconPixels></StrategicZoom>
<MinimapCombat><CellTiles>16</CellTiles><PulseSeconds>1.5</PulseSeconds><QuietSeconds>3</QuietSeconds><Max>32</Max></MinimapCombat>
<PlacementMinimap><MarkerPixels>8</MarkerPixels></PlacementMinimap>
<Formation><Spacing>1.2</Spacing><LooseTiles>4</LooseTiles><SlotTiles>12</SlotTiles><LostTiles>20</LostTiles></Formation>
<AutoWork><Radius>8</Radius><RepairBelow>0.9</RepairBelow><SliceTicks>10</SliceTicks></AutoWork>
```

The client-only numbers are presentation, but they live in `GameConfig.xml` with the rest so the parser checks them; `Formation` and `AutoWork` are balance and go in `gameplay.md`'s tables.

## 7. Wire and version

The order commands' `formation` flag changes two `[Command]` signatures, so `GameVersion.Protocol` becomes 16 and `bundleVersion` 0.16.0. Nothing else on the wire changes.

## 8. Testing and performance

- **EditMode:** target-line rules (which events draw, the hold, the cap, no line for an unnamed target); strategic zoom's threshold and hysteresis; minimap pulse binning and quiet time; the HQ-placement marker size per map size; formation slot layout (role order, rows, slots moved off rock) and the group speed; the movement stage's cap and slot steering (`SimFormationTests`); auto-work target choice, radius, slicing and the never-off-an-order rule (`SimAutoWorkTests`), including a 60-tile wall drag that gets built to the end; config parsing; `LeakTests` unchanged.

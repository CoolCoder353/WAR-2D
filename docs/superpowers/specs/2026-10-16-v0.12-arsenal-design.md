# v0.12 Arsenal: Design

**Status:** scope agreed with the owner on 2026-10-16 (brainstorm); open decisions 2 to 5 settled on 2026-10-17; HUD approved in Figma on 2026-10-17. **Built** on `release/v0.12` ([plan](../plans/2026-10-17-v0.12-arsenal.md)); the perf gate passes. Differences from this spec are listed in [known-issues.md](../../known-issues.md) (v0.12 Arsenal).
**Parent:** [roadmap](../../roadmap.md) §2 v0.12.

## 1. Goal

Six new units with distinct roles, and production reorganised so that units of a similar role come from the same building, each building makes one selected type at a time, and different roles come from different buildings.

**Agreed with the owner (2026-10-16):**
- **Units:** Scout (small, fast), Heavy (strong, slow), Artillery (**direct fire**), Sniper, Flamethrower, Suicide bomber (detonates **on contact and on a Detonate order**).
- **Production by role:** HQ makes Builders; Barracks makes Scout, Tank and Flamethrower; Heavy Factory makes Heavy, Artillery and Sniper; Siege Workshop makes Digger and Suicide bomber.
- **One type at a time;** switching a building's type **clears its queue**.

**Out of scope:** air units and anti-air, healers, stealth, transports, veterancy, unit upgrades.

## 2. Production

### 2.1 Buildings

| Building | Size | Makes | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HQ (`Base`) | 5×5 | Builder | Unchanged |
| **Barracks** | 2×2 | Scout, Tank, Flamethrower | Today's `SmallUnitSpawner`, renamed (enum, config type, art entry). Diggers move out of it. |
| **Heavy Factory** | 3×3 | Heavy, Artillery, Sniper | New. Large units leave from its edge. |
| **Siege Workshop** | 2×2 | Digger, Suicide bomber | New. |

All are blueprints built by Builders, with costs and upkeep in `GameConfig.xml`. `SpawnerRules.Produces` holds the mapping (the single source the server, HUD and tests read).

### 2.2 One type at a time

- `SpawnerData`'s mixed `queue` (a list of type bytes) becomes **`unitType`** (the selected type) and **`count`** (0–`MaxQueue`). A new producer starts on its first type.
- **Build time per unit**, not per building: each `<Unit>` gains `BuildSeconds` (Builder 2, Tank 1, Digger 1 keep today's rates; heavier units take longer), and the building's `SpawnRate` goes. A producer spawns one unit every `BuildSeconds` of its selected type, charging its cost at spawn as today.
- **Commands** (gated, validated, ownership-checked, *Playing* only, ≤ `MaxSpawnersPerCommand` buildings):
  - `CmdSetProduction(buildingIds, unitType)`: sets the type on each named producer that can make it and **clears its queue** (and its progress towards the next unit). Producers that can't make the type are skipped.
  - `CmdChangeQueues(buildingIds, delta)`: the queue's −, +, +10 and +50, for the selected type. The `unitType` argument goes.
  - The legacy `BuildingClicked` and `CmdDequeueUnit` are removed (the command card already uses `CmdChangeQueues`).
- `TargetSpawnerQueue` carries the type and the count. The rally point is unchanged.

### 2.3 HUD (Figma first)

- The command card's production area becomes a **type selector** (one icon per type the building makes, the selected one highlighted, its cost and build time shown) above **one queue row** (−, +, +10, +50, count). Several selected producers of the same kind share it; mixed kinds show no selector.
- The Builder's build menu gains Barracks, Heavy Factory and Siege Workshop beside Miner, Wall, Bomb and Radar.
- New icons for each unit type and the two new buildings, drawn in Figma and turned into `PixelIcons` bitmaps.

| Artillery | 120 | 40 | 14 | 4 | 2.5 | 220 | 6 | 7 | large (0.7) | Heavy | 6 | Splash 2, min range 4 |

### 3.1 Behaviour

- **Scout:** a normal unit with the largest sight; its job is to light targets for Artillery and Snipers (see §4.1).
- **Flamethrower:** each hit also damages every enemy unit and building within its splash radius of the target (§4.2).
- **Heavy:** large pathing class: needs 2 tiles of clearance, so 1-tile gaps and tight chokepoints stop it.
- **Artillery:** **direct fire**: needs a clear line like every unit (walls protect against it). Long range, splash, slow reload, a minimum range inside which it can't fire. Large pathing class.
- **Sniper:** very long range, one big hit, slow; a minimum range, so it is weak up close.
- **Suicide bomber:**
  - On an **attack-move** (or when idle and an enemy comes within its range) it targets the nearest enemy unit or building, runs at it, and **detonates on contact** (target within 0.8 tiles of its body edge).
  - A new **Detonate** order (`OrderKind.Detonate`, key `B`, rebindable, and a command-card button) detonates every selected bomber at once.
  - The blast uses the bomb's code path (`SimBombSystem`'s blast): every unit, building and wall tile in radius, **friends included**, with damage-table multipliers for the bomber (strong against buildings ×2 and walls ×3).
  - A bomber **killed before contact does not explode** (owner, 2026-10-16), so shooting bombers down is a real counter.

### 3.2 Armour classes

The damage table's single `Unit` target class splits into **`LightUnit`** and **`HeavyUnit`** (`TargetClass { LightUnit, HeavyUnit, Building, Wall }`, so `DamageTable.Classes` becomes 4). Each `<Unit>` gains `<Armour>Light|Heavy</Armour>`. Proposed multipliers (attacker → Light, Heavy, Building, Wall):

| Attacker | Light | Heavy | Building | Wall |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scout | 1.0 | 0.25 | 0.25 | 0.1 |
| Tank | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 0.5 |
| Flamethrower | 1.5 | 0.5 | 0.75 | 0.25 |
| Heavy | 1.0 | 1.25 | 1.5 | 1.0 |
| Artillery | 1.0 | 1.0 | 2.0 | 1.5 |
| Sniper | 1.0 | 0.5 | 0.25 | 0.1 |
| Suicide bomber (blast) | 1.0 | 1.0 | 2.0 | 3.0 |
| Digger, Builder | as today, with `Unit` copied to both classes |

The config parser keeps accepting `target="Unit"` as shorthand for both classes, so the existing entries still parse.

## 4. Combat changes

### 4.1 Fire only at what your side sees

Today a unit targets any enemy within range and line of fire, whether or not its side's fog grid sees it. That is harmless at Tank range (5) inside Tank sight (8), but Artillery (14) and Snipers (16) outrange their own sight, and a shot into the fog would reveal the target's existence. **Target search now also requires the target's fog cell to be visible on the attacker owner's grid** (`SimData.Visible`). Scouts, Radars and allies' shared vision become what lets long-range units fire.

### 4.2 Splash

- Units with `<Splash>` > 0 (Flamethrower, Artillery) queue a splash hit at the target's position along with the direct hit.
- A splash job (after the attack job, before `ApplyDamageJob`) walks the spatial hash around each impact and queues damage for every **enemy** unit (an owner on the attacker's attack mask) and building within the radius, scaled linearly from 100 % at the centre to 50 % at the edge, times the damage-table multiplier for each target's class. Allies are not hit (unlike the bomber's blast).
- Walls in the radius take splash too, through the wall-damage queue.
- `ApplyDamageJob` stays the single writer.

### 4.3 Minimum range

Units with `<MinRange>` ignore targets closer than it and keep searching.

### 4.4 Large pathing class

The large size class exists in the flow fields (`FlowSizeClass.Large`, its own filtered grid) but no unit has used it. v0.12 turns it on for Heavy and Artillery and fixes the edge cases `bug-review.md` lists: spawn asserts `SizeClass < FlowSizeClass.Count`, spawners search for a free tile with large clearance, and footprint push-outs respect clearance.

## 5. Content

- **Art:** an `ArtManifest` entry and a `RenderGen` generator for each new unit (8 facings) and building, the Barracks renamed; effects for the flame stream, the artillery shell and its impact, the sniper tracer and the bomber's blast (`EffectGen`). `WAR-2D/Art/Build all`.
- **Art review (owner, 2026-10-17):** the unit and producer sprites in `RenderGen.Arsenal.cs` ([samples](../../art/v0.12/index.html)) are approved. Heavy and Artillery draw on 2×2 canvases. The Barracks keeps the Spawner art with no flag (owner). The Builder unit is redrawn as a small bulldozer to match its icon (`RenderGen.Builder`); its first draft was rejected because the blade's hazard stripes ran along the blade at 45° facings, and the redraw (stripes in the unit's own frame) is approved (owner, 2026-10-17). Not in the atlases yet: the `ArtManifest` entries and `ArtBuild` cases come with the v0.12 build.
- **Effects and sound review (owner, 2026-10-17):** approved. Effects in `EffectGen`: `SniperTracer`, `ArtilleryShell` (2 frames), `FlameStream` (3×1 tiles, 4 frames), `ShellImpact` (4×4, 6 frames) and `BomberBlast` (4×4, 8 frames, the bomb's fireball). Sounds in `Assets/Art/Audio/sfx/`: `shot_scout`, `shot_flamethrower`, `shot_sniper`, `shot_heavy`, `shot_artillery`, `shell_impact`, `bomber_blast`. The first shot drafts were square waves and sounded like lasers; the approved shots are noise cracks with a falling pitch and the low-pass off (in this `Sfxr`, a low-pass below 1 cuts near 500 Hz). Not built into `Resources` yet: the `ArtManifest` entries, `ArtBuild` and `WAR-2D/Audio/Build SFX` runs come with the v0.12 build. The Tank's shot is still the square-wave sound.
- **Sound:** SFX parameter sets for each new weapon and the blast (`WAR-2D/Audio/Build SFX`), played through `AudioService` from the attack events the client already gets.
- **Replication:** no new message kinds; the client draws splash and blasts from the attack events and the unit type (it knows each type's splash radius from config).

## 6. Testing and performance

- **EditMode:** each unit's numbers parse; production (type switch clears the queue and progress, build time per type, refusals); splash (falloff, enemies only, walls, single writer); min range; fog-gated targeting (an enemy in range but in the fog is not targeted); the bomber (contact, Detonate order, friends in the blast); armour classes and the `Unit` shorthand; large-class pathing through a 3-wide choke and refused at a 1-wide gap; fuzzing for the new commands.
- **LeakTests:** splash and blasts never produce messages about units outside a client's interest.
- **PlayMode:** produce each unit from its building in a hosted match; switch a building's type; a bomber run on an enemy building.
- **Performance:** the perf gate's armies become a mix (e.g. 60 % Tanks, 15 % Scouts, 10 % Heavies, 5 % each of Artillery, Snipers and Flamethrowers). Splash and fog-gated search are the expensive parts; both are measured. The last gate's 20.9 ms p95 leaves about 4 ms.

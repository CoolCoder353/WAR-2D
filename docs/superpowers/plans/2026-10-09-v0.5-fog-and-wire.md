# v0.5 Fog & Wire — Plan

**Status (2026-10-09): closed.** Tasks 1–6 done; task 7 partly done (real loopback clients measure bandwidth; KCP tuning and the correction-cost work wait for gate numbers); task 8 not run. The open items moved to [known-issues.md](../../known-issues.md) for v0.6. Open questions settled on their defaults (tile→2×2 fog cells, 5 Hz, teams fixed at match start).

**Goal:** team fog of war drives replication interest; buildings join the unit encoder; transport tuned. Scope and exit criteria: [roadmap.md § v0.5](../../roadmap.md).

**Rules:** every task follows CLAUDE.md (server-authoritative, `SimCommand`s, `CommandGate`, config in `GameConfig.xml`). Each task ends with `tools/unity-compile.sh` + `tools/unity-test.sh EditMode` + `PlayMode` green, and its docs updated.

## Tasks

1. **Teams in the data model**
   - `teamId` on `ServerPlayer` (default: one team per player); a `TeamOf(ownerId)` lookup readable from Burst (`SimData` array by player index).
   - Combat target search skips allies. Win/loss is per team.
   - Lobby: host-only `Cmd_SetTeam` (gated, validated). Minimal UI only; full team lobby is v0.6.
   - Tests: allies never take damage; a team wins when all other teams are out.

2. **Fog grids (server)**
   - Per team: `visible` (current, refcount or bitset) and `explored` (memory of last-seen tile state) grids, map-sized.
   - New tick stage `SimVisionSystem` (Burst, after movement): stamps sight circles of units and buildings, ray-tested against rock (walls hook in v0.7). Sight radius per unit/building type in `GameConfig.xml`.
   - Emit per-team diffs of changed tiles at `Settled`.
   - Tests: rock blocks sight; explored keeps the last-seen state; allies share vision.

3. **Fog to clients**
   - Message: team grid diffs (RLE/bitset chunks), sent only to that team's connections; full snapshot on join/reconnect.
   - `ClientWorld`: fog overlay (texture updated from the grid) + explored-terrain memory rendering.

4. **Fog as replication interest**
   - Replace the camera-box interest in `ReplicationService` with: own team's units + enemies on tiles the team sees. Camera box remains only for correction fidelity, not permission.
   - Delete the client-supplied view-box trust (closes known-issues "No server-side visibility model").

5. **Buildings onto the encoder**
   - Add building Enter/Update/Leave to `Net/Replication/Messages.cs` (encoder + `ClientUnitStore`/a building store); filtered by the same fog interest.
   - Remove the `ClientPlayer` building SyncLists and `WorldStateManager.UpdatePlayerViews` path. Enemy buildings in explored-but-hidden tiles show as last-seen ghosts (client-side memory).

6. **Leak tests** (EditMode/PlayMode)
   - Hook the encoder's serialise path; assert every byte destined for client C concerns only: C's team entities, enemies on C-visible tiles, C's own resources, C's team fog. Never enemy resources, fog, or (stub) bombs.
   - Fuzz: random multi-team matches, check every tick.

7. **Transport tuning + real measurement** (clears 3 known-issues rows)
   - Perf gate measures real KCP loopback clients, not virtual ones (`Dev/PerfMatch.cs`).
   - Tune KCP (window, MTU, batching); chunk large messages.
   - Reduce melee correction cost: velocity-carrying corrections and/or hold-on-target hints in `ReplicationEncoder`.

8. **Gate**
   - `tools/perf-run.sh`, editor closed, 3 runs: 8 clients × 10,000 units within the bandwidth budget; tick budget unchanged from v0.4.
   - Results to `docs/perf/v0.5/`; update `architecture.md`, `known-issues.md`, `roadmap.md` status.

## Order

1 → 2 → 3 ∥ 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8. Leak tests (6) can start as a skeleton after 4 and grow.

## Open questions

- Sight on a tile grid vs. sector grid (cost at 80k units)? Default: tile grid, sight stamped from per-tile unit counts, not per unit.
- Fog grid diff rate: every tick or 5 Hz? Default: 5 Hz.
- Teams fixed at match start? Default: yes.

# v0.20 Onslaught: implementation plan

Written 2026-11-06 when the update started, from the [spec](../specs/2026-11-06-v0.20-onslaught-design.md). Built on `release/v0.20` (from `main` with v0.19). Every asset and HUD frame is already approved (asset review, Figma B1 to B3), so the work is the rules, the wire, the client drawing, the AI and the docs.

## Phases (one commit or more each)

1. **Renames and config.** `UnitType.SuicideBomber` → `Bomber`, `BuildingType.Bomb` → `Mine` (same enum values), `Siege/BombTriggerRadius` → `MineTriggerRadius`, `Siege/BombFriendlyDamage` → `MineFriendlyDamage`, `AlertKind.BombTriggered` → `MineTriggered`, art keys and sounds. The parser refuses the old names with an error naming the new ones. New keys: `<Combat>`, `<Burn>`, `<Artillery>`, `<Sniper>`, `<Effects>`, the `<Bomber>` leap keys (the v0.18 aim keys retired), per-unit `<BlastResist>` and `<FlatArmour>`, per-building `<BuildSeconds>`. The §11 starting values. Release 0.20.0.
2. **Shared combat rules** (`SimCombatSystem`): a hit carries its kind (direct, blast, fire) and an ignite flag; flat armour on direct hits with `Combat/MinHit`; blast resistance on every blast; burning per unit and building (by id index), applied once a tick through the same hit queue; the death's cause kept per id index for the wire.
3. **Bomber leap** (`SimBomberLeap`): army centre, leap trigger, a clear-line test (walls, rock, buildings), airborne state by id index (no target, no damage, no push, no orders), explode on landing; the v0.18 "most worth" aim retired. Any death on the ground sets it off (decay leaves it a sliver so the next tick's last gasp catches it).
4. **Artillery shells** (`SimShellSystem` data in `SimData.Shells`): fire over walls (a rock-only line of fire), lead aim, flight at `ShellSpeed`, landing blast with falloff, friendly share, no direct hit, 15 s reload.
5. **Sniper aim**: aim timer by id index, reset on movement, target change or loss; the shot fires when the aim completes.
6. **Gun turret**: 2×2, `BuildSeconds` (build work scaled per type), Bombers first.
7. **Wire** (`GameVersion.Protocol` 22, replay format 5): a `CombatBatch` per client from the settled world (status bits, leaps, aims, shells under the leak rule), Leave reasons with a death cause, a building burning flag. `LeakTests` cases.
8. **Client**: status, leaps, aims and shells in `ClientUnitStore`/`ClientWorld`; the approved effects registered in `ArtManifest`/`ArtBuild` and drawn; the sounds moved into `Assets/Art/Audio/sfx/` and played; B1 (burning marker, reload and aim bars) and B2 (hover-card lines, renamed icons, the 2×2 turret).
9. **AI**: `FightModel` and `balance-sheet.js` on the new rules; spotters; counters and turret counts; a 2×2 turret footprint.
10. **Manual and docs**: the drafted words into `manual-text.json` and `ManualText.Data.cs`; `gameplay.md`, `architecture.md`, `tuning.md`, `known-issues.md`, `roadmap.md`, `art/inventory.md`.
11. **Tests** (spec §13), then both suites once, then a code review, a security review and a performance review, with their findings fixed.

## Choices made while building (the spec left them open)

- **Blast radius keys:** a unit with `<BlastDamage>` is a Bomber; a unit with `<BlastRadius>` and no `<BlastDamage>` fires shells whose blast is its `<Damage>` (the Artillery).
- **Combat state by id index**, like the v0.18 bomber aims: burning, aiming and leaping live in arrays indexed by `NetIdAllocator.IndexOf(id)` with the id stamped, so the `Unit` component and the gather are unchanged.
- **Blast falloff:** a Bomber's and a Mine's blasts are unchanged (full damage across the radius, §2.5); a shell's falls to half at the edge (§4.2). Blast resistance applies to all three.

### v0.10: replays are a tick behind on units

The recorder's units go through `DeferredLane` (encoded during the next tick to keep the boundary inside the perf budget), so a recording's unit records are one tick later than its buildings and terrain. Harmless for playback; don't "fix" it by moving the recorder back onto the boundary (`docs/perf/v0.10/README.md`).

## v0.13 Bastion: built, reviewed, perf gate passes (headless host)

- **The Linux match ran headless.** On 2026-10-23 a Linux dev build ran as host and client (`-batchmode -nographics`) over Direct, driven by `tools/war2d`: each player's Gun turret killed an enemy Scout and wore down a Heavy (3.2 per shot, 8 × 0.4); each player's Jammer hid a Tank from the other player's Radar (the client was never sent it, the Radar owner's fog stayed dark there) while a unit outside the jam was seen, and a Scout 2 tiles away (within JamSight) did see it; an enemy turret inside the jam stayed a ghost. Not covered: the defences were placed with the dev `build` command (complete, free), not paid blueprints built by Builders, and nothing was drawn or heard (tracers, the turret head, rings, sounds) in a headless player. The Steam path wasn't run.

- **Fixed: nothing on the bottom and left border rows saw along them.** Fog cells are 2×2 tiles, and the vision stamp traced its line from the tile of the source's cell nearest the target, which for the bottom fog row (and the left column) is the border rock row; a line run along rock sees nothing. So a unit or a Gun turret one row from the bottom border saw no other cell on that row: a turret at (10,1) kept an enemy building as its target while an enemy Heavy stood 4 tiles east in the open, and the Heavy didn't see the turret either. Units were affected the same way (a pre-existing bug since v0.5's tile traces). When that trace is blocked and an end tile is rock, it is now tried again from or to that cell's nearest open tile, so a cell never sees less than before (`StampVisionJob.SeesCell`; `SimVisionTests.AUnitBesideTheBorderSeesAlongIt`, `SimBastionTests.ATurretByTheBorderSwitchesFromABuildingToAUnitEnteringRange`). Target choice itself was right: a turret (and a unit) re-runs the full search on its slice tick, units first, so a stored building or wall target never outlives a visible enemy unit in range.
- **Fixed: dev-placed turrets with a health override had no gun.** `WorldStateManager.DevPlaceBuilding(..., health)` copied only health, footprint and costs into its config, so a perf-harness turret got no `BuildingWeapon`. It now keeps sight and the weapon.
- **The owner's "Unpaid" state is exact.** The server's per-building unpaid flag reaches the owner (and only the owner) as a `BuildingRecord.Unpaid` record; the client's budget guess (`DefenceView.LooksUnpaid`) is gone.
- **The windowed perf gate** (a windowed player hung at start on 2026-10-23, see [perf/v0.13](perf/v0.13/README.md)) is signed off by the owner (2026-11-01).
- **Fixed in the v0.13 review (2026-10-23):**
  - *Builds of different versions could join each other.* `bundleVersion` was still 0.9.0, so the Steam version check let v0.12 and v0.13 builds (different wire formats) join each other, and Direct (KCP) had no check at all. The version is now 0.13.0, and `GameVersion.Protocol` (13) is checked for Steam (`SteamAuthRequest.protocol`) and Direct (`VersionHello`) joins, refused with "Different game version" (`SteamAuthTests`).
  - *JamSight was measured between fog-cell centres*, so a target 2.2 tiles away could be refused. It is now the distance between the two cells' closest tiles (`SimBastionTests.JamSightIsMeasuredBetweenTheCellsClosestTilesNotTheirCentres`). Cell granularity remains: with 2-tile cells, a unit can see a point up to about JamSight + 2.8 tiles away in a cell whose closest tile is within JamSight.
  - *Turret shots at walls sent no event*, so clients saw no flash, sound or "Firing" status. They now send an event with target 0 (`AWallShotIsABuildingAttackEventWithNoTarget`, `LeakTests.ATurretsWallShotReachesWhoMayKnowTheTurretNamingNoTarget`).

## Fixed after the first manual Linux match (2026-10-10)

The owner played the first full Linux match on 2026-10-10 and reported three issues:

- **Units got stuck on walls.** Flow fields run on 2×2-tile cells, and a cell with any wall tile is blocked. A unit standing on a floor tile inside such a cell (all along cave edges, and wherever separation pushed it) read "no direction" and headed straight at the goal, into the wall. It now steers to the nearest neighbouring cell that has a direction (`SimMovementSystem.NearestFieldCell`; `SimMovementTests.UnitBesideAWallInABlockedFieldCellStillGoesAround`).
- **Movement was jumpy.** Every correction (0.25-tile threshold, every 4 ticks; more in a jostling crowd) moved a unit's drawn position at once. Units are now drawn at `ClientUnitStore.Drawn`: the prediction plus the correction's jump, faded out over 0.1 s. The prediction itself still changes at once, so the server's copy stays bit-identical (`ReplicationTests.CorrectionsGlideInTheDrawnPositions`).
- **The menu battle had no shots or explosions.** Tracers were culled against `Camera.main`, which the menu camera isn't, and explosions only come from the server's `TargetPlayExplosions`. The battle now gives its view the menu camera and plays its own deaths' explosions (`LobbyTests.MenuBattleRunsAndIsGoneOnceHostIsPressed` waits for both).

- **The host couldn't pick a colour or a starting location** (owner reports, 2026-10-10). Owner decision: **reserved clearings**. The Figma change was approved on 2026-10-11 ([record](ux/v0.6-figma.md)): the host gets the colour and team pickers, every player claims a free clearing by clicking its marker on the lobby preview (the rest are handed out at start), and the HQ must be placed inside your clearing, which is ringed during placement (`LobbyTests.HostPicksAColourAndClaimsAClearing`, `PlacementRulesTests`, `MatchSettingsTests`).

## Fixed in the v0.6 bug run (2026-10-10)

Besides the owner's reports above:

- **Clicks on buildings never reached the world.** `GameInput.PointerOverUI` used `EventSystem.IsPointerOverGameObject()`, and `Map_2`'s camera has a `Physics2DRaycaster`, so the pointer counted as "over the UI" over every building collider and over the hidden hand-built `Walls` tilemap's collider (still active on generated maps, around tiles 29–91 × 45–96, a large part of a 256 map). Right-clicking an enemy building did nothing, and no box selection could start there. Physics raycaster hits no longer count as UI.
- **Clicking your own spawner raced box selection.** The spawner's `OnPointerClick` and `UnitCommander`'s box select both handled the same click, in no fixed order, and the box select cleared the building selection. `UnitCommander` now selects an own spawner on a click that hits none of your units.
- **The match-end wipe was silent.** `DeclareWinner` / `DeclareDraw` set _GameOver_, the tick stopped at the next boundary, and the wipe it then applied was never settled: no explosions, and no Leaves, so clients kept predicting the dead units behind the end screen. The tick now runs in _GameOver_ too (`SimBoundarySystem.RunsIn`), and explosions after _GameOver_ skip the fog test (`VisibilityRules.ShowsDeath`). At most 1,024 explosions go to one client per tick.
- **Players could join a running match, or a ninth player could join.** A late joiner had no team, colour slot, HQ or replication (and blocked the countdown in _PlacingHQ_); a ninth got a duplicate colour. The server now closes connections outside the lobby or beyond 8 players (`LobbyRules.MayJoin`), and the joining client says why.
- **Ctrl+number with nothing selected didn't empty the squad on the server.** The client cleared its copy and sent an empty chunk, which the server rejected as malformed; the squad bar then showed the old count. An empty chunk now means an empty squad.
- **Footprint writes raced the tick.** `MapStore.SetUsed` wrote the map's `Used` array (which the movement job reads) from the main thread whenever a building was placed or destroyed between ticks. Footprint changes are now queued (`MapStore.QueueUsed`) and applied at the next boundary; placement and spawn checks see queued changes at once.
- **`Cmd_SetTeam` ignored the lobby's rules.** The host could put players on teams 0–15 even in Free-for-all; it now takes only the choices a player could make (`LobbyRules.IsTeamChoiceValid`).
- **The player order didn't match the lobby.** `PlayerOrder` followed the server's dictionary order, which differs from join order after someone leaves, so the camera could start on another player's clearing. It is now sorted by net id, as the lobby is.
- **The selection box stopped growing** past about 31 tiles from where the drag began (the selection itself was right).
- Developer console commands threw when no match was running.

## UI

- **`com.unity.ugui` stays installed.** All screens are UI Toolkit. Building health bars moved to the instanced renderer in v0.7 and the `HealthBarUI` prefab is gone, but `GameInput` and the scenes still use uGUI's `EventSystem` (`InputSystemUIInputModule`), and `WAR2D.asmdef` references `UnityEngine.UI`. Removing the package means moving that over first.

## Developer tools

- **Fixed (v0.14): bots looked eliminated in the HUD.** Dev bots had no player object, so the top bar, diplomacy panel and gift dialog listed them as "Player (eliminated)". They are now Scripted AI seats (`GameCore.DevAddScriptedAi`) with a server-owned `ClientPlayer` (callsign, colour, `isAi`), shown alive until their HQ falls, and the server resends everyone's diplomacy row once a seat added mid-match has a simulation slot (`AiSeatPlayModeTests.AScriptedSeatIsAlivePlayerInTheHud`, `HudTests.GiftToEnemyMovesResources`).

- **Editor crash after `SimData` gains fields.** The first EditMode run after `SimData`'s layout changes can segfault while scheduling a tick job (v0.5: `GatherJob`; v0.6 Task 4: `SimCombatSystem`, in `AtomicSafetyHandle` handle extraction). Restarting the editor fixes it every time, so it is stale Burst code compiled against the old struct layout. Restart the editor after changing `SimData`'s fields, before running the tests.
- **The editor froze once during a PlayMode run** (2026-10-10, KDE Wayland, Vulkan): after `HudTests`' eighth match the main and render threads sat in futex waits with no CPU use for over 40 minutes, so the pipeline's requests timed out. Killing and reopening the editor fixed it, and the same suite then passed in full. If `tools/unity-test.sh PlayMode` stops reporting progress, check the editor window is visible before suspecting the tests.

## Networking

- **Direct play is UPnP or a tunnel, no relay.** _Steam players are unaffected: v0.9's Steam relay needs no open port._ Join-by-IP (the editor, `-direct`) reaches hosts on other networks only if their router accepts the UPnP mapping (or they forward UDP 7778 by hand) and has a public IPv4. Hosts on carrier-grade NAT, DS-Lite or behind a second router need a tunnel (see `playtesting.md`). The owner's own router (2026-10-11) answers UPnP but reports no external IPv4, so it falls in this group. There is no NAT punch-through.
- **A permanent-lease mapping can outlive a crash.** Routers that only take permanent leases (UPnP error 725) keep the UDP 7778 mapping if the game crashes before `OnStopServer`. Remove it in the router's UPnP page, or host again and leave normally. Direct hosts only.

## Project and config hygiene

Inert leftovers disclosed in v0.2 and scheduled to be cleaned up together.

- **Vendored Mirror components still call the legacy `Input` API** — `Components/GUIConsole.cs`, `Components/RemoteStatistics.cs` and `Components/Profiling/ToggleHotkey.cs` (the last via its own `GraphCanvas.prefab`). None are used in the shipped scenes, and the project is Input System only (`activeInputHandler: 1`), so they would throw if ever attached. Not shipped; leave vendored code alone.
- **Vendored Console `Demo/Scripts/DemoPlayer.cs` uses the legacy `Input.GetAxis`.** Demo only; not used by any shipped scene. Not shipped; leave vendored code alone.
- **Rebinding conflicts** _(code changed in v0.12, open until the owner confirms)_: Fast pan, Append, Point, Zoom and Drag pan no longer count (Shift is shared on purpose); the squad keys still do, named "Squad N (select)" (`Rebinding.NotRebindable`).
- **`Acceleration`** _(dropped in v0.12, open until the owner confirms)_: it was parsed and never read, so the setting and its config entries were removed.
- **Footprints ignore rotation.** `Footprint.Tiles` and the building sprite's placement assume a square footprint, so a rotated non-square building (Width ≠ Height) would cover the wrong tiles. Every building is square today, walls included (they are tiles).

## v0.7 siege review (2026-10-15)

Fixed:

- **Buildings outlived the match.** `SimContext.Dispose` destroyed units but not building entities, which only `DeclareWinner` / `DeclareDraw` removed. After a host stopped mid-match (and between PlayMode tests) the old spawners stayed in the ECS world, kept producing into the next match, and shared ids with its new buildings. This made `HudTests.ProductionPlusMinusChangesQueue` read a stale spawner and `CommandCardStopHalts` select extra units. `Dispose` now destroys buildings too.
- **Wall charges revealed the fog.** `CmdPlaceWall` tested each tile against the real map and charged before the sim's sight check, so with a budget for one wall, whether a visible tile got its wall told the player whether an unseen tile on the line was free. The command now only filters known bombs; the sim pays (`SimCommand.TryPay`) once the tile is seen and free.
- **A Repair order could find a hidden bomb.** Repair accepted an ally's bomb even when the ally didn't share vision, so the Builder's walk revealed it. It now needs the ally to share vision.
- **Builders kept working for a former ally.** `BuildWorkJob` checked alliance only when the order was given; it now stops a repair when either side attacks the other.
- **`WorkReach` was hard-coded** (and silently raised Digger reach from 0.6 to 1.6). It is `Siege.WorkReach` in `GameConfig.xml` now.
- **A new dig job inherited the old tunnel's last tile and progress.** `DigAt` and `DigProgress` reset when a unit's work target changes.
- **Buildings were one GameObject each on clients.** Fixed in v0.7: instanced (`InstancedBuildingRenderer`, one draw for buildings, one for construction overlays, one for health and progress bars), picked by tile (`BuildingPicker`). The SyncList-era handlers in `UnitCommander` (`BuildingList*`, `HealthList*`), `DataClient`, `BuildingDataClient`, `SpawnerClientManager`, `SpriteFrameAnimator` and the `HealthBarUI` prefab are gone.
- **Diggers couldn't be queued from the HUD.** The production buttons queued only the building's first unit type. The command card now has one production row per type the building makes (Figma `Command card – v0.7`).

Resolved by the v0.7 gate:

- **The flow-field sector cell size went from 2 to 1** (`OrderBook`), four times the sector fields. The v0.7 performance gate (2026-10-16, [docs/archive/perf/v0.7](archive/perf/v0.7/)) ran with it and passed.

Open (listed under "Open" at the top):

- **Terrain replication walks every changed tile.** `ReplicationService.SendTerrain` checks every tile changed since the match started, per client, on each vision tick, so the cost grows with the match.
- **Client positions creep after Stop.** Stopped units' predicted positions converge on the server's at about 0.15 tiles/s instead of snapping, so `HudTests.CommandCardStopHalts` (0.3 tiles over 1 s) can fail on timing when the prediction overshot when Stop arrived.
- **Authored maps (`Size = 0`) get no terrain on a pure client.** `WorldStateManager.OnStartClient` gives a pure client no `ClientTerrain` when `MapSize <= 0` (it can't rebuild the map); since 2026-10-18 the host's client gets one. Real matches always use generated maps.

- **Easy and Normal AIs can starve their own economy (2026-10-26).** In some runs an AI builds so many Miners and producers that building upkeep (50 to 54) exceeds its income (45), leaving it with 3 units for the rest of the match: it never reaches `AttackArmySize` and never attacks (or concedes). Which build order it reaches varies from run to run. This is what made `AiPlayModeTests.APrivateOneVsOneAgainstEasy…` fail about half of the time and `CoOpAHumanAndANormalAi…` fail now and then (match ended with no attack). The failure messages now print the AI's state (`PlayModeMatch.WaitUntil`'s `describe`). **Addressed by the AI overhaul (2026-10-30), to be confirmed in the bench:** every difficulty now plans its upkeep, buildings that cost upkeep stay under `Policy.BuildingUpkeepShare` (60%) of its projected income after the opening, and in a deficit Miners come first at every difficulty.
